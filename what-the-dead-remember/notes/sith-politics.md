## 1. Palpatine / Sidious

[P1] Palpatine, Senator of Naboo, became Supreme Chancellor in 32 BBY after Queen Amidala's motion of no confidence against Valorum. — Star Wars Databank, Galactic Republic, https://starwars.com/databank/galactic-republic
[P1] The election pitted Palpatine against Ainlee Teem of Malastare and Bail Antilles of Alderaan; Mas Amedda served as steward of the Senate. — Archivum wiki, Mas Amedda, https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Mas_Amedda [contested: single mirror summary, not confirmed against Wookieepedia]
[P1] His term expired but the Senate demanded he stay on to resolve the Separatist crisis; Jar Jar Binks moved the Emergency Powers grant. [contested: the 22 BBY date and link to the Military Creation Act are from background knowledge, not the source] — Star Wars Databank, Galactic Republic, https://starwars.com/databank/galactic-republic
[P1] LEGENDS: an "Emergency Powers Act" (24 BBY) removed Chancellor term limits. Do not use as canon. — Wookieepedia, Emergency Powers Act/Legends, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emergency_Powers_Act/Legends
[P2] UNVERIFIED: canon term structure (length of terms, the exact date his second term would have ended). The "two four-year terms" idea was not confirmed. If the story needs it, keep it vague: "his term had run out and the Senate would not let him go" is supported; a precise date is not.
[P1] Darth Plagueis in current canon is thin: Palpatine's tragedy speech in Revenge of the Sith (2005) is the first mention, and Plagueis appeared on screen in The Acolyte. — We Got This Covered / Supercluster summaries, https://wegotthiscovered.com/movies/star-wars-wiped-darth-plagueis-backstory-continuity/
[P1] LEGENDS: the 2012 novel Darth Plagueis shows Sidious killing Plagueis in his sleep. Not canon. — Star Wars Report, Book Review: Darth Plagueis, https://starwarsreport.com/2012/01/10/book-review-darth-plagueis/
[P1] Canon wrinkle: the novel Queen's Peril reportedly implies Palpatine was still an apprentice at the time of The Phantom Menace and killed his master after becoming Chancellor. — summary in the same We Got This Covered piece [contested: second-hand, author's reading of the novel]
[P2] UNVERIFIED: Sly Moore and Mas Amedda as Chancellor's office staff in 22 BBY (Amedda confirmed as Senate vice chair/steward only for the election, per above). Kinman Doriana's role not found.
[P2] UNVERIFIED: Anakin and Palpatine meeting frequency; Brotherhood, Queen's Hope, Obi-Wan & Anakin comic details. Searches returned nothing on these.
[P2] UNVERIFIED: cloud-the-Force/hiding from the Jedi, Death Star kyber origins, Geonosian design 22 BBY. Not retrieved.
[P1] 25 BBY: Palpatine travels to Jakku with his adviser Yupe Tashu and meets a young Galli. — Archivum wiki, 25 BBY, https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/25_bby

## 2. Dooku

[P1] The deletion is placed around the time of The Phantom Menace, about ten years before Obi-Wan looked for Kamino. — ComicBook.com (same URL) [contested: "during TPM" is a reporter's reading; exact BBY year not confirmed]
[P1] Attack of the Clones: the Kaminoan prime minister says Sifo-Dyas ordered the clone army at the Senate's behest; Jango Fett says he was hired by "a man called Tyranus." — Wikipedia-style summary, https://dorksideoftheforce.com/2021/03/17/how-did-the-clone-wars-happen/

[P2] LEGENDS: Plagueis manipulated Sifo-Dyas and Dooku killed him after the order. — IMDb FAQ for AotC, https://m.imdb.com/title/tt0121765/faq
[P2] UNVERIFIED: Dooku left the Order 32 BBY; the date, reasons, Count of Serenno, Raxus Address 24 BBY, Makashi curved hilt, "Jedi Lost" dates, Jango recruitment 32 BBY.
[P2] Ventress: trained by Ky Narec, fell to the dark side, became Dooku's informal assassin/apprentice; year not given in results. — Star Wars Databank, https://starwars.com/databank/asajj-ventress [contested: Legends vs canon versions differ; no BBY date found. [P2] UNVERIFIED: Qui-Gon as Dooku's Padawan, Obi-Wan as grand-padawan; this is canon but was not shown in these results.

## 4. Kamino / clones

[P1] Sifo-Dyas supposedly gave the Kaminoans the original control-chip concept; after his death Sidious and Tyranus kept the chips hidden from the Jedi Council. — Looper, Order 66 explained, https://www.looper.com/262759/star-wars-the-history-of-order-66-explained [contested: secondary source]
[P1] The Kaminoans were told the chips were a safeguard against renegade Jedi. Defective clones were more likely to be immune; Rex needed outside help to remove his chip. — same Looper article
[P1] The Bad Batch (Tech and others) did not respond to the chip the same way at Order 66. — netzwelt, https://www.netzwelt.de/serien/star-wars-the-bad-batch/191163-star-wars-the-bad-batch-klon-order-66-widersetzt-.html
[P2] UNVERIFIED: cloning start 32 BBY, ten-year accelerated aging, Rex born 32 BBY, Lama Su, Taun We, the 20 BBY Fives arc. Nothing confirmed in results. Rule of thumb for the story: in 26 BBY the first clones are about six years old, and the Jedi still do not know Kamino exists (supported by the archive deletion above).

## 5. Tatooine / Shmi

[P1] Anakin returned to Tatooine in 22 BBY; Shmi was taken by Tusken Raiders that year and died in his arms. Cliegg Lars had bought her from Watto, freed her and married her before then. — Star Wars Databank, Tatooine, https://starwars.com/databank/tatooine
[P1] Watto was tricked into a losing gambling game over Shmi; Cliegg bought her after Anakin left. — Wookieepedia, Mission to rescue Shmi Skywalker Lars, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_rescue_Shmi_Skywalker_Lars
[P1] Purchase date: 27 BBY per one wiki summary ("five years after TPM"). — Archivum wiki, Watto/Shmi, https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Watto [contested: the Legends Cliegg Lars page reportedly says 26 BBY (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cliegg_Lars/Legends); only the Looper piece gives a year in the other results]
[P1] Implication: in 26 BBY Shmi is very probably already freed and married, or about to be. Keep her off Watto's books.
[P2] UNVERIFIED: Owen's age and birth year (canon age gap to Anakin), Beru, Watto's finances, slave transmitters.
