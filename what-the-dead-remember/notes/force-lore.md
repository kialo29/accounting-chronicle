## A. Time, the Force, Mortis, World Between Worlds

[F1] In Rebels "A World Between Worlds" (1 BBY), Ezra Bridger unlocks a portal in the Lothal Jedi Temple, enters the World Between Worlds, and pulls Ahsoka Tano out of her duel with Darth Vader at the Malachor Sith Temple as it collapses. — Mission to the Lothal Jedi Temple / Lothal Jedi Temple (Databank), https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_the_Lothal_Jedi_Temple ; https://www.starwars.com/databank/lothal-jedi-temple
[F2] Inside the World Between Worlds Ezra meets the convor Morai (the bird tied to Ahsoka) and sees doorways between time and space; Sidious penetrates the place through the dark side and attacks; Ezra and Ahsoka return through the portals they entered, Ahsoka to Malachor, Ezra to Lothal; the Spectres lock the portal and the Temple sinks. — Time travel / Portal, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Time_travel ; https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Portal
[F3] [contested: Malachor date] Sources differ on gap (two vs three years); Malachor is placed 2-3 BBY, the rescue 1 BBY (Rebels S2 finale is 2 BBY... so "three years" in one summary is likely loose). Do not state a gap in prose. — Mission to Malachor, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_Malachor
[F4] Mortis: realm tied to the Force's balance; the Father, Son (dark side) and Daughter (light side) live there. Clone Wars S3 "Overlords", "Altar of Mortis", "Ghosts of Mortis" (a 2,000-year-old distress signal lures Anakin); S6 "Voices". — Mortis mirror/summary, https://starwars.com/databank/Son ; https://dorksideoftheforce.com/2023/10/25/is-peridea-the-planet-mortis-from-clone-wars/
[F5] The Son shows Anakin a vision of his future acts; Anakin accepts the Son's offer; the Father erases Anakin's memory of the visions. The Father dies by the Dagger of Mortis, the Son is killed by Anakin's lightsaber, the Daughter gives her life shielding the Father. — Son (Databank) and mirrors, https://starwars.com/databank/Son
[F6] UNVERIFIED: Ahsoka is killed on Mortis and revived when the Daughter's life force is transferred (the Daughter, dying, gives hers; Anakin brokers it). Confident from memory of "Altar of Mortis"/"Ghosts of Mortis"; the search summary only weakly supported it. Timing: Mortis arc is placed 20 BBY by timeline (memory).
[F7] UNVERIFIED (memory): No canon source has the Jedi Order treating "time travel" as a known practice in 26 BBY; Wookieepedia's "Time travel" page covers only the World Between Worlds (and Mortis-adjacent time anomalies, plus the Tales/Ahsoka-era references). Safe fic position: time manipulation via the Force is unknown to the Order.

## B. Order 66 / youngling survivors

[F8] Grogu was born c. 41 BBY, raised at the Coruscant Jedi Temple as an initiate, a youngling there during Order 66; Jedi Master Kelleran Beq carried him to safety; he lost the memory of the events through trauma. [contested: birth year is approximate] — Din Grogu / Grogu (Databank), https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Din_Grogu ; https://www.starwars.com/databank/grogu
[F9] Reva Sevander (Third Sister) was a youngling at the Temple during the 501st assault; survived by playing dead among her friends' bodies; later joined the Inquisitorius to kill Vader; Obi-Wan Kenobi (series, 9 BBY). — Reva Sevander, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reva_Sevander
[F10] Barriss Offee: Mirialan, Padawan to Luminara Unduli; birth year not confirmed in this pass (a mirror says 40 BBY). Her post-TCW fate varies across the mirrors found; treat any claim of Order 66 death on Felucia as UNVERIFIED. — Barriss Offee mirror, https://www.archivum.wiki/article/barriss_offee

[F11] Caleb Dume (later Kanan Jarrus): Padawan of Depa Billaba, who saved him during the Temple bombing and took him as Padawan; Billaba killed on Kaller by her own clone battalion (Order 66, 19 BBY), Caleb escaped through her sacrifice. Kaller details differ between the Kanan comic and The Bad Batch (reconciled in a later guidebook). The 33 BBY birth year was NOT confirmed in search. — Kanan Jarrus / Depa Billaba, https://www.starwars.com/databank/kanan-jarrus ; https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Depa_Billaba
[F12] Cal Kestis: a youngling at the Temple when the Clone Wars began (22 BBY), padawan when Order 66 hit (19 BBY, aboard Albedo Brave). Birth 32 BBY is UNVERIFIED (derived from fan age estimates of ~12-13 at Order 66; a Wookieepedia talk page disputes the age sourcing). By that arithmetic he would be about 6 in 26 BBY. — Cal Kestis, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cal_Kestis ; https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talk:Cal_Kestis
[F13] UNVERIFIED: Zett Jukassa (youngling killed in the Temple in ROTS, Anakin's Order 66 victim; Clone Wars 'Lost' novel/ comic sources give older padawan age) and Sors Bandeam (youngling in the Temple) birth years were not found. Do not assign ages.

## C. Training bond, Force-sensing

[F14] Canon: Yoda partly assigned Ahsoka to Anakin to teach him non-attachment; a strong teacher-student bond grew (Databank). Ahsoka later describes him as brother, then father figure. — Anakin Skywalker, https://www.starwars.com/databank/anakin-skywalker ; Ahsoka Tano, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ahsoka_Tano
[F15] No dedicated canon "master-padawan Force bond" mechanic was found. UNVERIFIED (memory): the Legends 'Padawan braid / training bond' telepathic link is Legends; canon screen shows only emotional sensing (Anakin and Ahsoka sensing each other's distress; Kanan/Ezra holocron lessons, Kanan sensing Ezra at distance in Rebels). Treat any telepathic bond as fic invention or label LEGENDS.
[F16] Legends-sourced detail seen in the Ahsoka/Legends page: Cad Bane exploits Anakin and Ahsoka's bond; this is on a Legends page, so do not use as canon. — Ahsoka Tano/Legends, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ahsoka_Tano/Legends
[F17] The Chosen One page exists as canon reference for prophecy wording; exact wording was NOT retrieved. UNVERIFIED (memory): canon phrase is the Chosen One who would 'destroy the Sith and bring balance to the Force'; Qui-Gon believed it was Anakin (TPM). — Chosen One, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chosen_One

## E. Lightsaber combat

[F22] Form V (Shien/Djem So): Shien variant permits reverse grip; Ahsoka favoured it with one or two sabers (Wookieepedia, Form V). Djem So = strong blocks/parries into counters; deflects blasters but limited mobility. Per a Wookieepedia forum thread, lightsaber-form specifics are partly Legends-derived; check page-level canon tags. — Form V, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V
[F23] Jar'Kai: Ahsoka paired lightsaber and shoto; page is the /Legends page, so Legends status for details. [contested] — Jar'Kai/Legends, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar'Kai/Legends
[F24] Official Databank documents Ahsoka's lightsabers (Clone Wars/Rebels). — Ahsoka's lightsabers, https://www.starwars.com/databank/ahsokas-lightsabers
[F25] UNVERIFIED (memory): Vader's style is Form V Djem So-leaning; Obi-Wan Soresu; Dooku Makashi; Windu Vaapad; Ahsoka's reverse grip appears from the TCW film (2008), age 14. Not confirmed in this pass.

## G. Healing, ghosts, prophecy

[F32] Halls of Healing: wing of the Coruscant Temple; healers used the Force plus crystals and amulets; after the Clone Wars rebuilt under Chief Healer Cilghal (page tagged canon). Vokara Che as Chief Healer placed Anakin and Obi-Wan into healing trances after Geonosis and Zigoola. The Force healing and hibernation-trance pages carry Legends tags, so trance mechanics are [contested]; Whiphid healing trance exists on its own page. — Halls of Healing, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Halls_of_Healing ; Vokara Che, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vokara_Che
[F33] Qui-Gon learned Force-spirit survival from a Shaman of the Whills, died (TPM) before finishing, spoke as a voice, later completed it; Yoda learned via Qui-Gon during Dagobah exile; Obi-Wan trained after the Clone Wars. Qui-Gon appears to Obi-Wan on Tatooine. Yoda and Obi-Wan vanish at death; Qui-Gon does not. — Force spirit, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_spirit ; Qui-Gon Jinn, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qui-Gon_Jinn
[F34] Chosen One: destined to destroy the Sith and restore balance to the Force; Qui-Gon's dying words (TPM) point Obi-Wan to Anakin. — Obi-Wan Kenobi, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi
[F35] Force ghosts return only to give guidance and do not change events (fandom page note, weak source). — Forum:KB:force ghost, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Forum:KB:force_ghost
