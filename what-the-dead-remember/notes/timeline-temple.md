## B. Jedi Temple life

[T23] The Gathering: initiates go to Ilum's crystal caves; the caves test each person's fears; a kyber crystal responds (usually blue or green); then they build the saber under Professor Huyang. Ilum temple access works on a seventeenth-day cycle, when the sun rises over it. Age and Yoda-led detail: UNVERIFIED (Clone Wars "The Gathering" is the source to check). — Gathering, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gathering ; Jedi Temple (Ilum), https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_(Ilum)
[T24] Rank of Initiate begins with communal training in clans. One source says clans of twenty initiates; another says travelling groups of 6 to 8 younglings escorted by a higher-ranked Jedi. Named canon clan: Kybuck Clan. UNVERIFIED: Bear Clan, Wolf Clan, Hawkbat Clan. — Jedi Initiate, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate ; Kybuck Clan, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kybuck_Clan
[T25] After the Initiate Trials, a youngling must be chosen as Padawan by a Knight or Master, who then gives individual tutelage. UNVERIFIED: age-13 cutoff and Service Corps wording. — Jedi training, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training
[T26] Padawan braid: symbol of apprentice status, worn in the hair (or on the head, by species); removed on promotion to Knight or on banishment. — Padawan braid, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order ; What is a Padawan?, https://www.starwars.com/news/what-is-a-padawan
[T27] Younglings wear standard Jedi apparel: sandy-coloured tunic, brown belt and boots (one wiki's account). Basic saber classes (moves, safety) are taught by Temple saber masters before Padawan selection. — Unidentified Jedi youngling, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Jedi_youngling
[T28] Lightsaber forms: canon status of the named forms not confirmed. A Form V mention and Jar'Kai appear in a talk-page discussion; Legends lists seven forms and says initiates first learn Form I. UNVERIFIED: Soresu/Obi-Wan, Djem So/Anakin, Shien/Ahsoka, Makashi/Dooku, Vaapad/Windu, Ataru/Yoda (all [memory] and widely used in canon; check the Databank). — Talk:Lightsaber combat/Legends, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talk:Lightsaber_combat/Legends
[T29] Cin Drallig: canon. Jedi Master, Battlemaster and Chief of Security of the Temple during the Clone Wars; led the Temple Guard and taught saber combat; said to have trained Obi-Wan and Anakin; died defending the Temple during Order 66 (killed by Darth Vader) alongside two Padawans. — Cin Drallig, https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cin_Drallig ; https://www.starwars.com/databank/cin-drallig
[T30] Jocasta Nu: Chief Librarian of the Jedi Archives, controls access to the Holocron Vault; survived the early Purge and was later killed by Vader (canon comics). — Jocasta Nu, https://www.starwars.com/databank/jocasta-nu ; https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jocasta_Nu
