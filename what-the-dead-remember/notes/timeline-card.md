## Spans and ages
- Anakin: died 4 ABY, woke 26 BBY: THIRTY years back. Twenty-three years in the suit. Nineteen at Geonosis (his arm, four years from now). Palpatine has groomed him since he was nine, six years.
- Obi-Wan: back four years. In his old life he sat on the Council about three years. Naboo was six years ago (he was twenty-five). The Death Star was destroyed within a day of his death.
- Ahsoka: back seven years. Order 66 to Malachor: sixteen years. Mortis to Malachor: seventeen years. Left the Order at sixteen. She woke seven years ago aged three.
- Dooku: a Jedi for about seventy years. The war is four years away. Naboo six years ago.
- The Gathering party: SIX younglings (Ahsoka, Kessa Ruun, Tam Dorreth, Ilyo, Brannick Sel, Wix). Ahsoka is "sixth of six". Five come out; she stays. The cipher is Aurebesh shifted SIX.
- Thirty-one is the Temple-wide total of younglings who shared the dream.
- Hawkbat Clan: twenty children aged nine to eleven (ten, mostly).
- The Lost Twenty: Dooku's bust is the twentieth.
- Units: metric only (centimetres).
- The training-saber class on day 1: Anakin was NOT late; Drallig called "Tano"; she righted the grip before Anakin reached her; he said "Thumb along the emitter"; the hand-over-hand correction was Brannick's; Ilyo pushed his shoulder; the hall is on the fourth level; after class Drallig asked "Was there something?" and Anakin said "She holds it backwards". - Dead-drop: a sealed sample box behind the third waterfall, under the moss.
