# Canon reference (consolidated). Chapter writers: treat this as authoritative. Do not search.

Disney canon unless marked. Where canon is silent the choice below is the book's choice; keep to it.

## Dates (BBY = before the Battle of Yavin). The book opens early in 26 BBY.
- Anakin born 41 BBY on Tatooine. Freed and taken by Qui-Gon 32 BBY (aged 9). Obi-Wan's Padawan since 32 BBY. **15 now.** Knighted 22 BBY in canon; Clone Wars 22–19 BBY; becomes Vader 19 BBY; dies at Endor 4 ABY, aged 45. Right forearm lost to Dooku (Geonosis, 22 BBY); left arm and both legs lost to Obi-Wan (Mustafar, 19 BBY); lungs and skin burned on the lava bank. 23 years in the suit.
- Obi-Wan born 57 BBY. Qui-Gon Jinn's Padawan; killed Maul on Naboo and was knighted 32 BBY (aged 25); took Anakin that year. **31 now**, short beard, Form III Soresu. Dies on the Death Star 0 BBY aged 57. His return-point knowledge ends there.
- Ahsoka born 36 BBY on Shili (mother Pav-ti). Found by Plo Koon aged 3 (33 BBY) and brought to the Temple. **10 now**, an initiate (youngling). Canon: Anakin's Padawan at 14 (22 BBY); left the Order 20 BBY after the Temple bombing frame-up (Barriss Offee); Siege of Mandalore and Order 66 19 BBY (removed Rex's chip); "Fulcrum" for the rebellion; fought Vader on Malachor 3 BBY aged 33. Her return-point knowledge ends as the Malachor temple collapses. (She was in fact pulled out by Ezra in 1 BBY; she does NOT know this.)
- Padmé born 46 BBY. Queen of Naboo 32–28 BBY (two two-year terms). **Senator of Naboo since 28 BBY, aged 20 now**, on Coruscant. Her security chief: Captain Typho. Bail Organa (Alderaan) and Mon Mothma (Chandrila) are also senators now. Current Queen of Naboo: Réillata. Dies 19 BBY, Polis Massa, after the twins' birth.
- Palpatine born 84 BBY. Senator of Naboo; Supreme Chancellor since 32 BBY (6 years in; the law's term limit is approaching and he has not yet secured the "crisis" that will keep him in office; do not state exact term rules). Darth Sidious. Staff: Mas Amedda (Chagrian Vice Chair of the Senate), Sly Moore (Umbaran aide). Office in the Senate Office Building; apartment in 500 Republica. Guarded by blue-robed Senate Guards (not red guards yet). In canon he has mentored Anakin since 32 BBY, with regular private audiences. Lair in the Works (industrial district) for Sith business.
- Dooku born 102 BBY (**76 now**). Qui-Gon's master; left the Order after Qui-Gon's death (32 BBY), killed Yaddle, erased Kamino from the Archives with Sifo-Dyas's codes, became Count of Serenno and Darth Tyranus. Hired Jango Fett as the clone template (32 BBY). Publicly a respected ex-Jedi philanthropist and critic of Senate corruption; his open Separatist turn (Raxus Address) is 24 BBY, two years away. Curved-hilt red saber, Form II Makashi. Bust among the "Lost Twenty" in the Archives.
- Sifo-Dyas: Council member who ordered the clone army from Kamino in 32 BBY and was killed the same year on Oba Diah by Pykes hired by Dooku; the Pykes kept his lightsaber. Officially "lost". The Jedi do not know Kamino exists; the entry is gone from the Archives.
- Kamino: clones growing since 32 BBY (accelerated aging, 10 years to maturity); in 26 BBY the first batch, including Rex (born 32 BBY), are 6 years old and look 12. Inhibitor chips are implanted at the embryo stage and sold to the Kaminoans as a safeguard against rogue Jedi. Lama Su (prime minister), Taun We, Nala Se.
- Maul: bisected on Naboo 32 BBY; alive and mad on Lotho Minor until 20 BBY. Obi-Wan knows this (learned 20 BBY) and that he killed Maul on Tatooine in 2 BBY. Ahsoka knows Maul lived (Mandalore 19 BBY; Malachor 3 BBY). Anakin knows.
- Pykes: Oba Diah, Pyke Syndicate; Lom Pyke leads them.
- Jango Fett: on Kamino, aged 40, son Boba aged 6; takes outside jobs. Mandalorian armour, jetpack, twin pistols. Zam Wesell is his occasional partner (Clawdite shapeshifter).
- Asajj Ventress: NOT in Dooku's service yet. Keep her out. Grievous: not yet. Caleb Dume (Kanan Jarrus) born 33 BBY, **7 now**, youngling. Cal Kestis about 6. Barriss Offee: birth year not canon; the book makes her **12**, an initiate a clan above Ahsoka. Grogu: born 41 BBY, a youngling here, physically a toddler; cameo only if it serves.
- Jedi High Council, 26 BBY: Yoda, Mace Windu, Plo Koon, Ki-Adi-Mundi, Saesee Tiin, Shaak Ti, Eeth Koth, Adi Gallia, Oppo Rancisis, Even Piell, Coleman Trebor, Depa Billaba. Never list them all in prose.
- Dated nearby canon: Obi-Wan and Anakin's Carnelion IV mission was 29 BBY (Anakin, 12, considered leaving the Order; Palpatine took him into the undercity). Nothing is dated in 26–25 BBY on Coruscant; the book is free.

## The Jedi Temple
Ziggurat on Coruscant with five spires (central Tranquillity Spire; four Council towers; the High Council Chamber is the circular room atop its tower, twelve seats). Processional Way and main entrance (Gate Master Jurokk). Room of a Thousand Fountains (vast greenhouse, meditation, waterfalls). Jedi Archives (Chief Librarian Jocasta Nu; busts of the Lost Twenty; Holocron Vault). Halls of Healing (Chief Healer Vokara Che, Twi'lek; Jedi doctor Rig Nema). Training halls and dojos; the Temple hangar; dormitories for initiates (clan dorms); shared quarters for master and Padawan (two small rooms off a common room). Temple Guard (masked, yellow double-blade pikes). Battlemaster Cin Drallig heads lightsaber instruction and Temple security; he taught Obi-Wan and Anakin and will die under Vader's blade in 19 BBY.

Younglings/initiates: brought to the Temple as infants or toddlers, raised in **clans** of up to twenty (book uses **Hawkbat Clan** for Ahsoka's cohort, aged 9–11, and **Bear Clan** for the youngest, including Caleb Dume). Sand-coloured tunics, brown belts, boots. Classes: Yoda (meditation and the Force), Drallig and his assistants (training sabers: low power, they sting and bruise), Jocasta Nu (Archives), plus galactic history, languages, maths, mechanics, astrogation, diplomacy. Senior Padawans assist in initiate classes. Initiate Trials around 12–13; a youngling not chosen as a Padawan by about 13 goes to the Service Corps. The Gathering: initiates around 10–12 go to Ilum with Yoda aboard the *Crucible* (architect droid Huyang), enter the crystal caves at sunrise, must return before the ice door freezes at sunset (reopens at the next sunrise), each finds a kyber crystal that calls to them (usually blue or green), then builds a saber with Huyang. Ahsoka's canon crystals: green (a second, yellow-green shoto later).

Padawans: braid behind the right ear (Anakin's: a thin braid, short hair). Jedi Trials for knighthood later. Padawans take advanced classes and go on missions with their master; the Council assigns missions, often at the Chancellor's or Senate's request. Lightsaber forms: I Shii-Cho (basics), II Makashi (Dooku), III Soresu (Obi-Wan), IV Ataru (Yoda), V Shien/Djem So (Anakin; Vader's version is heavy, single-handed overhand power strokes, brutally efficient, economical, no flourish; Shien reverse grip is Ahsoka's), VI Niman, VII Vaapad (Windu). Younglings learn Shii-Cho. Jar'Kai = two blades (Ahsoka).

Force practice in this book (canon-adjacent, keep consistent): a master and Padawan share a **training bond**, a felt presence and mood at the back of the mind, which can be shielded; Jedi can sense emotion and the dark side's stain on a person or place; mind-touch/probe (gentle, with consent in healing; forced, by Sith); mental shielding is learned discipline, and Sith shielding feels different to a Jedi: old, scarred, built over years; visions and prophetic dreams ("always in motion is the future"); healing trances; Force choke and lightning are dark side. Qui-Gon's spirit exists but is not reachable yet. The Order knows nothing of time travel; none of the three know how they came back. Mortis (20 BBY): the Father, Son and Daughter; the Daughter gave her life-force to revive Ahsoka; Morai the convor (green-feathered owl-like bird) is the Daughter's creature and follows Ahsoka; the Father erased Anakin's vision of his future.

## Coruscant
Senate District: the Senate Building (great dome, floating pods), Senate Office Building (Chancellor's office, red-walled, wide window over the city), 500 Republica (towers of the powerful), Senate Apartment Complex (Padmé's quarters), Monument Plaza. CoCo Town (Collective Commerce district) with Dex's Diner (Dexter Jettster, Besalisk, old friend of Obi-Wan's; he knows Kaminoans). Uscru District (Galaxies Opera House up top, the Outlander Club below). The Works: derelict industrial district where Sidious keeps a lair. Speeder lanes, levels, the undercity. Air traffic, the Temple visible across the skyline from the Senate.

## Tatooine, Shmi
Shmi was bought from Watto and freed by moisture farmer Cliegg Lars about 27 BBY and married him; she lives at the Lars homestead near Mos Eisley/Anchorhead with Cliegg and his son Owen (about 16). In canon she is taken by Tusken Raiders in 22 BBY and dies in Anakin's arms; he massacres the camp. Anakin knows this. Beru Whitesun is not yet at the homestead. The book: Obi-Wan has allowed Shmi and Anakin to exchange letters for about three years (against custom); a Temple courier handles them.

## What each returned person knows (and does not)
**Anakin (from 4 ABY):** everything above through his death; Order 66 from the inside (he led the 501st into the Temple; killed Drallig; the youngling who asked "Master Skywalker, there are too many of them. What are we going to do?"); Fives and the chips (he was there when Fives died; as Vader he learned the full truth); Mustafar from both sides; the suit; hunting Jedi with the Inquisitors; Bespin; that Obi-Wan lived as "Ben" in the Jundland Wastes (learned when he searched Tatooine after Yavin); Luke and Leia; Malachor (he believed Ahsoka dead there, or did not let himself check); the Emperor's lightning; throwing Sidious into the reactor shaft; Luke taking off the mask; "You were right about me. Tell your sister... you were right." Does not know how he came back. Does not know Obi-Wan's ghost stayed with Luke.
**Obi-Wan (from 0 BBY):** Palpatine is Sidious, Dooku is Tyranus, Kamino, Sifo-Dyas (learned 19 BBY), Maul's survival; the clones turned (he believes on Sidious's order; he does NOT know about the chips); Mustafar (he took Anakin's limbs and left him burning; "You were my brother, Anakin. I loved you." / "I hate you!"); Padmé's death and the twins; Tatooine, Owen and Beru, watching Luke from a ridge; Leia's rescue 9 BBY and the cracked helmet ("Anakin is gone. I am what remains." / "Then my friend is truly dead. Goodbye, Darth."); the Death Star ("If you strike me down, I shall become more powerful than you can possibly imagine."); Satine. Does not know Vader turned back. Does not know Ahsoka's fate after 19 BBY. Does not know Luke's future.
**Ahsoka (from 3 BBY):** the war; Anakin as master ("Skyguy"/"Snips"); Rex, the 501st; Barriss's bombing and her expulsion; Mortis and the Daughter; the Siege of Mandalore; Order 66 from the receiving end; the chips (she removed Rex's); the Empire; Inquisitors (the Grand Inquisitor was a Temple Guard, a Pau'an); Kanan Jarrus = Caleb Dume, blinded by Maul on Malachor; Ezra; that Vader is Anakin ("Anakin Skywalker was weak. I destroyed him." / "Then I will avenge his death." / "Revenge is not the Jedi way." / "I am no Jedi."; and, through the cracked helmet: "Ahsoka..." "Anakin..." "I won't leave you. Not this time." "Then you will die."). Does NOT know of Luke or Leia, Padmé's children, Obi-Wan's death or life on Tatooine, Vader's redemption, or that she was rescued. She believes Anakin is gone for good.
