# Outline — What the Dead Remember

Each paragraph is the beats that MUST land. POV in brackets. "Ambiguous" means the pre-reveal rules in the bible apply.

## PART ONE — THE RETURNED

**Ch 1 — The Weight of Breath [Anakin]** (4,500w) Open at Endor: the Death Star's hangar, the mask coming off, Luke's face, "tell your sister you were right", light. Then: Anakin wakes in the dark in a narrow bed, breathing without a machine, with two hands. The catalogue of a fifteen-year-old body: legs, lungs, skin without scars, no pain, the smell of the Temple. The training bond is there and alive on the other end: Obi-Wan, asleep, thirty paces away. Anakin slams a Sith-built shield across it on instinct. A chrono and the skyline tell him when he is: six years before the war. Obi-Wan knocks, bearded, thirty-one, gentle: "Nightmare?" The first lie. Alone till dawn, Anakin decides: hide everything, change nothing yet, find out why he is here. He does not feel the convor; he feels, far off, something ring like a struck bell and does not know it is a child waking.

**Ch 2 — Too Many of Them [Anakin]** (4,200w) First morning. Obi-Wan is wrong: warmer, teasing, touches his shoulder, says "my friend"; mentions Anakin is assisting Master Drallig with the Hawkbat Clan's saber class at the tenth hour, as he does every tenday. Anakin walks into a hall of twenty children with training sabers and the Temple burns behind his eyes: the 501st at his back, the Council chamber, the boy's question. He functions. He corrects grips, kneels to a girl's height, hears Drallig's voice and sees Drallig's death. A small Togruta initiate in the back row holds her saber reversed; Drallig calls her "Tano"; Anakin's knees nearly go. He corrects her grip; his hand shakes; she watches his hand, not his face. He does not correct the grip, in the end. Refresher after, retching. He tells himself: she is ten. She is a child. Malachor is seven years ago and twenty-three years from now.

**Ch 3 — The Youngling [Ahsoka, ambiguous]** (3,900w) Hawkbat dormitory before dawn; Ahsoka was awake all night, since the Force "rang like a struck bell" and she sat up in her bunk with her heart going. The daily performance of being ten: Kessa Ruun, the tunic that doesn't fit, the Bear Clan boy Caleb Dume who follows her, Master Plo's visit and "little 'Soka". The saber class: Padawan Skywalker came in late, white, and looked at the children as if counting them; he knelt to Kessa; his hand shook on Ahsoka's wrist and he looked at her reversed grip a long time and let it be. She tells herself nothing. She adds a line to a list she keeps in Togruti under her mattress (the reader does not see the line). Night: she goes to an empty hall and practises with two training sabers reversed, and the feeling of being watched from the gallery. Last line: something came into the Temple that night, and it was wearing Skywalker's face. Keep every future noun out.

**Ch 4 — The Garden [Anakin]** (4,300w) Every tenth day the Chancellor sees the boy. Anakin walks through the Senate District into the office of the man he served for twenty-three years and killed. Palpatine, warm, fifty-eight, pours juice, asks after Obi-Wan, talks politics (Outer Rim trade bills, Senator Amidala's "energetic" reform faction with Organa and Mothma, Count Dooku's elegant pamphlets, his own tiresome term limit), and all the while walks in the boy's mind, as he has for six years. Anakin has lived inside this mind; he knows the touch. He builds a surface: a fifteen-year-old's resentments, speeders, his mother's letters, an invented crush. Beneath it, a wall. He feels Sidious find the wall and pause. One slip: Anakin says "my Master" with a cadence that belongs to another room. Palpatine's eyes. Walking home, the feeling of a door tried. Flash: lightning, the throne room, the weight of the Emperor in his arms.

**Ch 5 — Tea and Lists [Obi-Wan, ambiguous]** (3,900w) Obi-Wan's morning: tea, the skyline, a mental ledger of the small things he has changed in his Padawan's life (the letters; the saber classes; never a whole day with the Chancellor). The bond went opaque two nights ago, like a door closing; he has told no one. Yoda summons him: a shadow has come into the Temple, new, close, the dark side's stain; the Council will look. Obi-Wan deflects. He watches Anakin spar in Drallig's hall and sees him lose on purpose, and sees one parry that stops his breath: he has seen it before and will not think where. A green convor sits on the ledge outside the Council spire; he feels something old and bright and his eyes sting without permission. Alone: "Not again. Whatever this is. Not again."

**Ch 6 — Letters from Tatooine [Anakin]** (4,200w) The Temple courier brings a letter; Obi-Wan hands it over as if it were ordinary. From Shmi. Free, married to Cliegg Lars, a stepson called Owen, three years of letters Anakin has never read. He reads his own fifteen-year-old letters in the courier's archive and cannot bear his own handwriting. Flash: the Tusken camp, her face. He writes back as a boy and nearly fails. He starts the datapad list in Vader's cipher: *Things that are different.* Letters. Obi-Wan's warmth. The Tano child's grip. The bell. Three theories: his memory is rotten; this was never his timeline; someone else is here. He decides to test memory against record in the Archives: Carnelion IV matches; his own file matches; then, almost idly, he enters "Kamino". No entry (expected). But the system shows prior queries for the same string: one, two years ago. KENOBI, O. He sits very still. Rationalisations (Sifo-Dyas's disappearance; Qui-Gon's old friends) and none of them hold. He does not add it to the list. He adds it to the list.

**Ch 7 — The Dream the Temple Heard [Anakin]** (4,500w) Night: Anakin cannot shield in sleep. Full flashback: the march up the Processional Way, the 501st, Jurokk at the gate, Drallig's duel in the fountains room, the Council chamber, "Master Skywalker, there are too many of them." The dream spills through the Force; younglings wake screaming across the Temple; Yoda sits up in the dark. Morning: the Halls of Healing summon Padawan Skywalker; Obi-Wan comes; Vokara Che asks to walk the dream with him. Anakin builds a false dream in seconds (Tatooine, sand, his mother's hands, a slaver's whip) over the real one and lets her in. She finds the fake and, beneath it, "a wall, old and scarred, as if built over years"; she says so to Obi-Wan in front of him. Windu in the corridor after: "Something has come into this Temple, Padawan. If you know what it is, now is the time." Anakin lies to his face. Flash intercut: the window, Palpatine's hand, Windu's hand falling away into the dark.

**Ch 8 — The Night the Temple Screamed [Ahsoka, ambiguous]** (3,900w) She was inside the dream too: she saw a hall she knew, fire, white armour she will not name, and a boy's face; she woke on the floor between the bunks. Every other child cried. She did not; she went to the refresher and was sick in silence, then held Kessa. Caleb Dume would not let go of her sleeve. Master Plo comes to the clan; she asks him, carefully, whether the Council would believe a youngling who had a dream that was true, and gets a Master's answer and a long look. She resolves to get close to Skywalker: the Gathering list; she manoeuvres (a word to Master Plo, a word to the clan-mother) to be on it. At the fountains she sees Kenobi and Skywalker together and Kenobi looks at her a beat too long, and she looks away first. Night practice again; this time she finds a mark on the gallery rail that was not there before: someone sat here. Keep every future noun out.

**Ch 9 — The Count on the Holonet [Anakin]** (4,400w) Master Vos-Kerrick's politics class; the holonet shows Count Dooku speaking at a Serenno cultural institute, exquisitely critical of Senate corruption; the Padawans debate; Anakin says nothing and sees a kneeling man and two blades crossed at his throat and a voice saying "do it". Then Drallig's advanced class: Anakin paired with Orrin Bellas, 17. Anakin loses on purpose, twice. Bellas calls him slave boy, says something about Kenobi. Instinct: one-handed overhand, Bellas's saber across the hall, the blade stopped a finger from his throat, the hall silent. Drallig: "Where did you learn that?" "I don't know, Master." Obi-Wan in the doorway; through the thin bond something that is not surprise. Night, the Temple roof: the convor, three metres off, green, looking at him. "Someone is watching me," he thinks. "Not Palpatine. Something older." End of Part One.

## PART TWO — THE WATCHERS

**Ch 10 — The Archive Ghost [Anakin]** (4,300w) Anakin goes after the log. Jocasta Nu; the courtesy of librarians; what a Padawan may and may not see. He slices what he may not (Vader's hands know the Archive's bones). Obi-Wan's Kamino query, 28 BBY, and a second query the same week: "Sifo-Dyas". Then, because he cannot stop, Initiate Tano's file: a healer's note, 31 BBY, age five: persistent night terrors, would not describe them, asked the healer "whether a dream can be a memory". No recurrence. He builds a theory board on the datapad and hates every line of it: Obi-Wan? the child? both? neither? He decides to test Obi-Wan with something small and safe: at supper he mentions "the Duchess of Mandalore", a name this boy should not know. Obi-Wan, mild: "Who told you about Satine?" No clue either way. Deceit as a game begins. Anakin realises he is enjoying it and is ashamed.

**Ch 11 — The Chancellor Takes Tea [Obi-Wan, ambiguous]** (4,000w) Palpatine asks for Kenobi, alone. Obi-Wan sits across from the Chancellor and is courteous for an hour: the boy seems withdrawn; the boy had a bad night; the boy might benefit from accompanying the Chancellor to a Senate function; the boy will be on Ilum with Master Yoda for the Gathering. Displeasure under warmth. Afterwards Obi-Wan goes to the Archives and asks Jocasta, lightly, who has been reading access logs, and learns: Padawan Skywalker. Why would Anakin look for a planet that does not exist? He goes to Dex's Diner and asks about Kaminoans again, and Dex says what he said two years ago and asks why a Jedi keeps asking about a people nobody has seen. Night: the dream Obi-Wan has every few months, a desert, a farm, a boy he is not permitted to approach. He wakes and does not write it down.

**Ch 12 — Interlude: The Door [Palpatine, then Dooku]** (3,000w) Sidious in the Works. The boy's mind was a garden he walked in for six years; now a wall, old and scarred, built by someone who knows Sith discipline. He has taught that discipline to two people. One is dead on Naboo. He summons Tyranus by hologram; Dooku denies touching the boy and is believed almost entirely. Sidious orders him to Coruscant for the anniversary reception of the Battle of Naboo, to look at the boy himself, and remarks on Kenobi's obstructions and the Gathering. Dooku on Serenno after: Qui-Gon's boy; the Order he left; his own reasons. The Senate strand: Amidala's reform faction is a nuisance; a crisis is wanted; the Outer Rim is ready to be made unhappy. A third possibility Sidious does not say aloud.

**Ch 13 — Ilum [Anakin]** (5,500w) The *Crucible*, Huyang, Yoda, six younglings including Tano, and a Padawan escort Obi-Wan arranged. Yoda watches Anakin: "Changed, you are. Grown." The ice door at sunrise; the children go in; Anakin stands with Yoda and counts them out. Five return. The sun goes down the mountain. Yoda: "Her path, her own it is." Anakin goes in as the door freezes; Yoda does not stop him. The caves work on him too: Padmé, lava, a bridge of ice that looks like a reactor gantry. He finds Tano before a wall of ice, ten years old, shaking, a green crystal in her fist, and in the ice he can feel what she is seeing: a black shape with a red blade. He reaches into her mind to pull her out and meets a shield, and the shield is his, the one he built and taught. "Who taught you that?" She turns. "You did."

**Ch 14 — The Long Night in the Ice [Ahsoka, open]** (5,500w) Ahsoka's POV opens fully for the first time. Snowed in till sunrise. Two children in the cold and two soldiers in their heads. She tells him where she comes from: Malachor, the mask, "then you will die". He tells her Endor, Luke, the mask coming off, that he died in the light. She does not believe him; Vader says what he needs. They test each other with things only they know: the Daughter, Mortis, Snips, Skyguy, Rex, Fives. She tells him about the chips; he knew. He tells her about Luke and Leia; she did not know; Padmé's children lived; she cries for the first time in seven years. They set the rules: lie to everyone else; find out if Obi-Wan is back (she says she'd know; he tells her about the log). Dawn; the door thaws; Yoda waiting, the crystals. "Found her, you did." A pause. "Found you, she did." Ahsoka's crystal is green.

## PART THREE — TWO AGAINST THE DARK

**Ch 15 — Snips [Anakin]** (4,200w) Back on Coruscant. Mechanics of an alliance between a Padawan and a youngling who must never be seen together: a dead-drop behind the third waterfall; a thin bond, made deliberately, kept faint. Humour comes back for the first time, and so does the hard conversation: she asks what he did in the Temple, and he tells her, exactly, including the children, including Drallig. Flash: Christophsis, a fourteen-year-old with a stick saying she's the new Padawan. She does not forgive him. She works with him anyway. They draw up what each knows that the other does not and realise how much of it is about Obi-Wan.

**Ch 16 — Of All the Children [Obi-Wan, ambiguous, heavy]** (4,000w) Yoda reports Ilum: the Padawan went into the caves after the Tano child against custom and came out at dawn carrying her. Obi-Wan notices them now, everywhere: in the fountains room, in corridors, never speaking. Of all the children in the Temple, that one. Plo Koon talks about little 'Soka and Obi-Wan says nothing true. He sets Anakin a Soresu drill and watches him fail it on purpose, then says it plainly: "You are hiding something from me. I would rather you lied to me well than badly." He gives Anakin the one thing he can: the day off and a speeder. Night: the dream of the desert and the farm and the boy, and this time the boy turns round. Obi-Wan wakes and says a name he does not say aloud and the reader does not hear.

**Ch 17 — The Reception, Part One [Anakin]** (4,500w) The sixth anniversary of the Battle of Naboo; the Chancellor's reception in the Senate; the heroes of Naboo invited: Kenobi, his Padawan, Senator Amidala. Ahsoka's advice at the dead-drop: don't look at her like she's already dead. Arrival; Palpatine's hand on his shoulder; and across the room, Count Dooku, seventy-six, in black, come "to honour Qui-Gon's memory". Dooku's courtesy to Obi-Wan ("my student's student, and his"); Dooku's hand not quite touching Anakin, and the Force-touch beneath it. Anakin's hatred flares and he clamps it, a second too late. Dooku's eyes. Palpatine watching both. Then the doors, and Padmé Amidala, twenty, in senatorial blue.

**Ch 18 — The Reception, Part Two [Anakin]** (5,000w) She remembers him ("Ani?"), kind, curious, twenty; he is fifteen and has her entire life and death in his hands. Every sentence he does not say. She mentions she sent a message to the Temple years ago and never heard; he says the Temple is strict. He decides the only way to save her is to never be loved by her, and is cold-polite, and watches it land as a small hurt and a puzzle. He gives her one true thing disguised as a boy's odd remark: never trust anyone who says the Senate needs a stronger hand. She laughs. "You sound like Captain Typho." She touches his arm and goes. Terrace; he comes apart in silence. Obi-Wan finds him and stands beside him. "I know." "You don't." "No. I suppose I don't." Across the terrace, Dooku watching, and Palpatine watching Dooku.

**Ch 19 — Interlude: The Count's Report [Dooku]** (3,000w) Dooku's night. He touched the boy's mind and found hatred aimed at him with the precision of a history: the hatred of a man for someone who has taken something from him. Qui-Gon's boy has never met him. To the Works: "The boy is not yours, and he is not mine. Someone has had him." Sidious: "Then we will find out who." The plan: remove the master, the way the Jedi always lose their masters; test the boy in grief; and bring back a recording. Dooku contacts Jango Fett and the Pykes; Oba Diah; a Republic survey team; a request will reach the Council through the proper channels. Dooku's private thought, the first he has not shared: that he does not entirely wish to know who has had the boy.

**Ch 20 — The Shape of the Trap [Ahsoka, open]** (4,000w) Ahsoka's Temple life as a spy with a child's access: she listens through Master Plo; she watches Barriss Offee, twelve and earnest, in the next clan up, and cannot decide whether to hate a twelve-year-old; she keeps Caleb Dume from a fall on the stairs and holds him too long. The children are children: a water fight in the fountains room, a scolding, a game of "Jedi and Sith". Then the news through Plo: the Council is sending Kenobi to Oba Diah to negotiate for a Republic survey team held by the Pyke Syndicate; the request came through the Chancellor's office. Ahsoka knows the Pykes. Dead-drop: "Oba Diah. Pykes. It's a trap. Who asked for him by name?"

**Ch 21 — Not This Time [Anakin]** (4,400w) Anakin asks Obi-Wan straight, in their quarters: "Why did you look for Kamino in the Archives?" "Who told you I did?" "The logs." Obi-Wan's story is excellent: Sifo-Dyas, Qui-Gon, Dooku's old friend, a Master lost and a name on a shipping manifest. Anakin cannot tell. The assignment: Obi-Wan goes to Oba Diah; Anakin stays, by Obi-Wan's insistence, the first time he has ever been left. Anakin's fury is old and borrowed and he knows it. Obi-Wan at the hangar: "Whatever you are hiding, I would still have you at my back. But not this time." The phrase lands like a blade: Malachor; Ahsoka's voice; a cracked mask. Flash: "I won't leave you. Not this time." Obi-Wan's ship goes up through the traffic lanes and Anakin stands in the hangar with a ten-year-old twenty metres away pretending not to look at him.

**Ch 22 — Oba Diah [Obi-Wan, ambiguous, very heavy]** (4,200w) The Pykes, their spires, Lom Pyke's courtesy; the survey team's leader Deshan Orr; the negotiation Obi-Wan conducts beautifully while thinking: the Pykes. Of course it would be the Pykes. On a gantry, a man in Mandalorian armour whom Obi-Wan recognises and should not. The trap springs: the hostages are bait; droids and Pyke guards; Soresu holds and holds and cannot hold forever; a shot through the side; the gantry; the floor coming up. He thinks of a desert and a farm, and says, to a boy who is not there, "I'm sorry, Anakin. For all of it." Darkness, and in it, the bond, thin as a thread, and someone pulling on it hard enough to hurt.

**Ch 23 — The Theft [Anakin]** (5,500w) The bond tears open: Obi-Wan's pain comes through like a blade. Ahsoka at the drop: "Go." A ten-year-old lies to the Temple Guard and the hangar officer with a straight face and slices the hangar log; Anakin takes a courier shuttle out through the lanes and into hyperspace with Vader's hands on a boy's controls, the seat too big, the harness adjusted twice. Oba Diah: descent, the spires, the gantry. He fights as Vader: no flourish, heavy, overwhelming, economical; a Pyke lifted off his feet by the throat; droids cut in half; a Mandalorian helmet watching from above, recording, then gone on a jetpack. He finds Obi-Wan bleeding under a stair. A boy carrying a man with the Force. The survey team freed in passing. The shuttle; the medkit; Anakin's hands working; Obi-Wan half-conscious, having seen him fight. Anakin, not thinking, in the voice of a man: "Stay with me, Ben."

## PART FOUR — THREE

**Ch 24 — Ben [Obi-Wan, open]** (5,500w) Obi-Wan's interior unlocked for the reader at last: Tatooine, the ridge, the Death Star, the four years, all of it. No one alive knows that name. "Where did you hear it?" Anakin: "From my son." Luke. The conversation in hyperspace between two dead men. Obi-Wan learns: Vader killed the Emperor; Luke; the mask; the light. Anakin learns: Obi-Wan watched Luke grow; the cracked helmet, "then my friend is truly dead"; the Death Star; what four years of this have been. Then Mustafar, which they never said: "You left me to burn." "Yes." "I hated you for twenty years." "I know. I heard you." "I'm sorry." "So am I." Nothing is healed. Something is begun. The ship drops out over Coruscant.

**Ch 25 — Three [Anakin]** (4,500w) Council inquiry: a stolen shuttle, a Padawan on an unassigned mission; Windu; Yoda's long look; Obi-Wan lies to the Council fluently and the lie holds. Night, the Room of a Thousand Fountains: Obi-Wan and the Tano child. Her last sight of him was a hologram; his last of her, a girl walking down the Temple steps. "Master Kenobi." "Ahsoka." The three lists come out. Pooled knowledge: the chips (Obi-Wan did not know; Cody; he has to sit down); Kamino; Sidious's suspicion; Dooku's touch at the reception; a helmet that recorded everything. The three understand that the Sith now know the boy is wrong, and that the Council is still hunting the shadow. Anakin: "So we're all lying to everyone." Obi-Wan: "Yes. Try to do it well."

**Ch 26 — What Remains [Anakin]** (4,500w) The Vader chapter. Night; Anakin sleeps unshielded for the first time because Obi-Wan sits in the room. Long flashback braid: the first breath in the suit; the bacta tank on Mustafar; hunting Jedi with the Inquisitors; the Temple purge from the inside; the day a bounty hunter said the name Skywalker; Bespin; the inside of the hate, what it was for, what it bought. Intercut with the present: Obi-Wan in the chair, seeing it through the bond, not looking away. Morning: Obi-Wan looks ten years older. "I saw." "I know." Anakin asks if he can still be looked at. Obi-Wan looks at him.

**Ch 27 — The Council Must Not Know [Obi-Wan, open]** (4,200w) Obi-Wan's four years in full: waking at twenty-seven; the first sight of the eleven-year-old; deciding not to tell Yoda and why; the letters; the Chancellor; the Archives; the list of the dead; trying for Qui-Gon's voice and getting silence. Now, Yoda asks him directly about the shadow. Obi-Wan gives him a shaped truth: the boy had a vision of the Order's end and Obi-Wan believes it. Yoda's answer and what it costs them both. Padmé has sent a message asking after the Padawan; Obi-Wan tells Anakin. The first argument of the three: kill Palpatine now (Anakin); not yet (Obi-Wan: what he knows of the Senate, of Dooku, of what happens to Jedi who kill Chancellors); the chips first (Ahsoka). No resolution.

**Ch 28 — The Daughter's Bird [Ahsoka, open]** (4,000w) Ahsoka's view of the three: she is ten and they talk over her head and she hates it; she is the only one who knows the chips from the inside and Maul's survival from the Siege. Her choice about Plo Koon: she tells him a shaped truth, that she has dreams of the future and they are true and one day the Council will have to trust a child; "then I will remember that you said it." Barriss: Ahsoka chooses kindness and it costs her. Caleb. The convor comes to her on the roof and she understands at last: the Daughter's life is in her; the Daughter did this. She tells Anakin: "It's her." "Then why me? I was there when she died." "Because you were there." She does not say what she suspects about Obi-Wan's return, because she does not know.

**Ch 29 — Interlude: The Recording [Palpatine]** (3,000w) Sidious watches Jango's helmet footage: a boy of fifteen lifting a Pyke by the throat without touching him; the overhand stroke; the economy. He has never seen that style. He knows its grammar: it is his. Someone has made the boy a Sith's instrument, and there are two Sith. Dooku beside him, silent, watched. A third possibility: someone they thought dead. Sidious changes the timetable: ten years was the plan; the plan is now. Politics: the crisis will be brought forward; the Outer Rim; a bill; Amidala's name on a list. He summons the boy to a private supper. He says, to the frozen image, almost fondly, "Who taught you that, my boy?"

**Ch 30 — Supper [Anakin]** (5,500w) The invitation cannot be refused without confirming. Obi-Wan and Ahsoka prepare him; none of it helps. The Chancellor's private dining room; Sly Moore at the door; two people at a table who each know the other is more than he seems and neither able to say so. Palpatine goes for the mind, fully, for the first time. Anakin lets him into the vault of the fake boy, and deeper, and for one second lets him touch a mind that has held Sidious by the throat over a reactor shaft, and then closes. Palpatine smiles. "You have grown, my boy." "Yes, Chancellor." Flash: lightning; the lift; the weight. Anakin walks home through the Senate District and sits down on a bench and cannot stand. Obi-Wan finds him. Ahsoka is on the roof when they get back, and the convor is with her.

**Ch 31 — Shmi [Anakin]** (4,800w) Obi-Wan invents a mission and takes him to Tatooine. The Lars homestead; Cliegg; Owen, sixteen, suspicious; Shmi alive, free, grey at the temples, holding him. He is fifteen and she is alive and he cannot tell her anything and tells her he is all right. He fixes the vaporators. He sits on the ridge where he will bury her and does not. At night he walks toward Tusken country and stops, and comes back, and Obi-Wan is waiting. "You didn't go." "No." Obi-Wan looks at the homestead for a long time and Anakin watches him look, and knows what he is seeing: a ridge, a boy, nineteen years.

**Ch 32 — The Negotiator [Obi-Wan, open]** (4,200w) The Council's inquiry into Oba Diah: Obi-Wan reports a Mandalorian hunter and a Pyke operation with money behind it; Windu's scepticism; Yoda's silence. Dooku sends condolences and an invitation to Serenno "to discuss Qui-Gon's legacy"; Obi-Wan sees the hook and declines in writing. The three plan the long game: Anakin's cover above everything; Sifo-Dyas's missing-person case reopened by Obi-Wan as a route to Kamino and the chips; Padmé kept clear of the Chancellor by means they can manage; Dooku. Obi-Wan and Windu, alone: Windu says the boy frightens him. Obi-Wan says: "He frightens me too. That's why I'm staying."

**Ch 33 — The Ledger [Anakin]** (4,500w) The initiates' saber class again; he teaches; the children are children; for the first time he does not see the Temple burn when they raise their sabers, and then he does, and it passes. Letter to Shmi. Then Obi-Wan gives him the list of the dead: every Jedi who dies in the purge and where, four years of handwriting. "I kept it so I would not forget what we are preventing." Anakin adds the names Obi-Wan does not have: the ones Vader killed after. Ahsoka adds hers. Three lists, one datapad. "Will you tell Yoda?" "When he can hear it." Then, finally: "You said you loved me. On the bank." "I did." "Say it where I can hear it." He does.

**Ch 34 — What the Dead Remember [braided: Ahsoka, Obi-Wan, Anakin, then Sidious]** (4,000w) Four short movements. Ahsoka on the roof at dawn with the convor and a green crystal, ten years old and thirty-three. Obi-Wan with tea and a message from Senator Amidala about a committee she is forming on Outer Rim labour practices, and a list he does not open. Anakin in the hangar, fifteen, whole, fixing a courier shuttle's compensator with a youngling handing him tools and pretending not to be anyone. Then the Works: Sidious and Dooku before the frozen image of the boy's face. "Find out what happened to Maul." Dooku: "Maul is dead, Master." "Then find out what else is." End.
