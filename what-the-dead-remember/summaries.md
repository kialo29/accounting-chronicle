
### Ch 1
Endor: Vader's suit fails in the Death Star hangar; Luke drags him to the ramp and removes the mask. Anakin sees his son's blue eyes unfiltered, says "You were right about me. Tell your sister... you were right," and dies in white light. He wakes in the dark in a narrow Temple bed, breathing without a machine, with two flesh hands, no scars, no pain, a Padawan braid, a boy's cracking voice. He feels Obi-Wan asleep through the training bond thirty paces away and slams a Sith shield across it on reflex, then realises the slam itself betrayed him. A chrono and the pre-war skyline place him six years before Geonosis. Obi-Wan knocks, bearded, thirty-one, and asks "Nightmare?"; Anakin says "Yes, Master", his first lie, and Obi-Wan, after an oddly long look round the room, believes him. Till dawn Anakin decides: hide everything, change nothing yet, find out why he is here and whether he is alone. Far below, something rings like a struck bell.

### Ch 2
First morning. Obi-Wan is wrong: he smiles too fast, teases, puts a hand on Anakin's shoulder and calls him "my friend"; Anakin cannot tell whether his memory has rotted or the man has changed, and resolves to write it down. Obi-Wan reminds him he assists Drallig with Hawkbat Clan's saber class every tenday. Anakin walks into a hall of twenty loud, bored children and functions, correcting grips and kneeling to a Rodian girl's height, while Order 66 cuts in: the 501st on the steps, the Council chamber, Drallig's eleven-second death, the boy's question. In the back corner a small Togruta initiate holds her training saber reversed; Drallig calls her "Tano" and Anakin's knees nearly go. He kneels, takes her wrist, his hand shakes; she watches the hand, not his face, says one word. He lets go without correcting the grip; she turns it back round. Afterwards he retches in a refresher: she is ten, she is a child.

### Ch 3
Same day, Ahsoka Tano. Awake since the struck-bell note in the night, she lies in her Hawkbat bunk, feet not reaching the mattress end, naming nothing. She performs ten: lets Kessa Ruun fix her lek bead, wears a tunic a quarter too small, steers Caleb Dume round a pillar and holds on a half-second too long, cannot look at Barriss. Master Plo finds "little 'Soka"; she asks whether he felt anything in the night (he did not) and he recalls her asking at five whether a dream can be true. In saber class the Padawan arrives late and white, counts the ranks, kneels to Kessa with the wrong smile; his hand shakes on her wrist, studies her reversed grip, asks "Always?", she says "Yes", he lets it be; she reverses it again. She adds an unseen eighth line to her Togruti list. At night she practises two reversed sabers in a dim dojo, feels watched from the empty gallery, senses something new and hard-shielded in the Temple. Last line: something came in that night wearing Skywalker's face.

### Ch 4
Nine days after waking, Anakin keeps his standing tenth-day audience with the Chancellor. Obi-Wan walks him to the Senate Office Building, lingers at the foot of the steps and visibly cannot go up; Anakin recalls his failed Council request to end the audiences. In the red office Palpatine pours muja juice and talks Outer Rim trade, Amidala's reform bill with Organa and Mothma, Dooku's privately printed pamphlet on Senate ethics, and his own approaching term limit, while his mind-touch strolls the boy's mind. Anakin feeds it a built surface (Drallig's forms, a Mobquet speeder, a generic mother-hollow, an invented crush on Padawan Mirren Dask) and lets a boy's flush cover what Padmé's name does to him. The touch reaches the Sith-built wall, stops, and is pleased. Anakin says "my Master" in Vader's kneeling cadence; Palpatine's eyes hold on him. Walking home: the sense of a door tried; a flash of Endor lightning and the Emperor's weight.

### Ch 5
Obi-Wan's morning, day ten: tea, the Temple's shadow on the skyline, and his private "accounts" of small changes: the letters (three years), the initiate classes (two), never a whole day with the Chancellor, and yesterday's failure to climb the Senate steps. The bond closed ten nights ago "like a door"; he has told no one. Yoda summons him to the eastern spire: a shadow, new and close, the dark side's stain in the house; the Council will look. Obi-Wan deflects without lying outright ("I would not call what I have felt in him that"); Yoda notes he felt Obi-Wan's door close too. In Drallig's hall Anakin loses to Bellas on purpose; one one-handed parry stops Obi-Wan's breath; Drallig says he never taught it. Alone below the Council chamber, a green convor sits on the ledge; something old and bright makes his eyes sting. "Not again. Whatever this is. Not again."

### Ch 6
Afternoon of day ten. Obi-Wan hands Anakin a courier letter "as if it were ordinary": from Shmi, free, married to Cliegg Lars, stepson Owen sixteen, the vaporators, the fence; she keeps his last letter in her apron. In Anakin's memory there were no letters. He opens the courier archive and reads three of his own boy-letters (twelve, thirteen, fourteen) and cannot bear the handwriting. A flash of the Tusken tent interrupts and resolves nothing. He writes back as a boy, tearing off three sheets before one holds. He starts the datapad list in Vader's cipher (keyed to the first respirator's serial; nobody alive can read it): letters, Obi-Wan's warmth, the Tano child's grip, the bell; three theories. In the Archives he tests memory against record: Carnelion IV matches; his own file matches. Jocasta Nu passes. Idly he enters "Kamino": no record, but one prior unfilled query, KENOBI, O., two years ago. Every rationalisation fails. He does not add it to the list. He adds it.

### Ch 7
Night of day ten: exhausted, Anakin sleeps unshielded. Full Order 66 dream: the wet Processional Way, the 501st's boots behind him, Jurokk's question at the gate, the Temple Guard, Drallig killed in the dark Room of a Thousand Fountains, the Council tower stairs, Bear Clan younglings hiding among the chairs, a boy glad to see him, the blue blade. He wakes to the Temple ringing with crying children (Hawkbat and Bear among them), feels Yoda sit up in the dark, and knows he did this, twice. Obi-Wan sits on his bed, hand on his shin. Morning: Halls of Healing. Vokara Che reports thirty-one younglings shared one dream centred near his level, asks to walk it; he forges a Tatooine dream (sand, his mother's hands, a slaver's whip) in four seconds. She finds it, then the wall, and tells Obi-Wan in front of him: "a wall, old and scarred, as if built over years"; "Who taught you to shield?" "No. He didn't." Windu in the corridor: "now is the time." Anakin lies. Obi-Wan never asks about the wall.

### Ch 8
Ahsoka POV, night of day ten/eleven through day eighteen. She is inside the shared dream: a stair she knows, a burning hall, ranks of white she will not name, a boy's face at the end of a blue blade asking a question. She wakes on the floor between bunks; all sixteen Hawkbat children cry; she does not, is sick silently in the refresher, then holds Kessa. Bear Clan is brought in to be counted (thirty-one); Caleb Dume grips her sleeve. Plo comes; she asks whether the Council would believe a youngling whose dream was true; he gives a Master's answer, recalls her asking at five (she lies: "I don't remember"), looks at her a long time. Master Sen announces Kenobi's Padawan will accompany Yoda's Gathering; Ahsoka manoeuvres (Plo, Sen, Kessa as cover) onto the list, seventh of seven. At the Fountains Kenobi looks at her a beat too long; she looks away first. Night practice: dust on the gallery rail worn where someone sat.

### Ch 9
Anakin's POV. In Master Vos-Kerrick's politics class the holonet shows Count Dooku, 76, in black at a Serenno institute, delivering a seductive indictment of Senate corruption ("patience is a sentence"). Bellas, Mirren Dask and the others argue; Anakin stays silent, flashes to the Invisible Hand (Dooku kneeling, two blades, Palpatine's "Do it"), then answers as a boy. In Drallig's advanced class he throws two bouts to Orrin Bellas, 17, who calls him "slave boy" and says Kenobi only kept him for a dying promise to Jinn; instinct takes over: a one-handed overhand sends Bellas's saber the length of the hall and stops a finger from his throat. Drallig: "Where did you learn that?" "I don't know, Master." Obi-Wan in the doorway; the thin bond carries something that is not surprise. Drallig sets morning sessions until the Crucible leaves; Bellas gets Bear Clan. At night on the Temple roof a green convor sits three metres off, watching, and does not fly. "Not Palpatine. Something older." End of Part One.

### Ch 10
Part Two opens on day twenty. After Drallig's dawn penance Anakin goes to the Archives. Jocasta Nu, courteous and exact, grants him his master's training-materials access record and refuses the general query log. At terminal nine, beneath the Soresu commentaries, Vader's hands slice the Archive's bones through a nine-hundred-year-old maintenance account in four minutes, and it frightens him. He finds Obi-Wan's full Kamino record (28 BBY, terminal four, forty minutes, null) and, four days later, "Sifo-Dyas", filled, read for eleven minutes. He tries an innocent story; the four days break it. Unable to stop, he opens Initiate Tano's file: healer's note, 31 BBY, age five, night terrors, "whether a dream can be a memory", no recurrence. He refuses the conclusion. On a spire landing he writes a four-line theory board (Obi-Wan / the child / both / neither) and hates each. At supper he drops "the Duchess of Mandalore"; Obi-Wan, mild: "Who told you about Satine?" Unreadable. Anakin realises he enjoyed the game and is ashamed. Ilum in three days.

### Ch 11
Obi-Wan POV. Palpatine summons Kenobi alone two days before the Crucible leaves. Over an hour of tea Obi-Wan never touches, the Chancellor probes about the boy: withdrawn at the last audience, the thirty-one younglings' dream, the Bellas bout, whether the boy is "given enough to do" and is "alone". He proposes Anakin accompany him to the Battle of Naboo anniversary reception in some weeks; Obi-Wan parries with Ilum and "the Council", and the Chancellor notes six years of refusals. In the Archives Jocasta tells him Anakin asked for the query log, was refused, and that night opened it from terminal nine under a dead librarian's account; the trace took eight days; she has logged it, not yet reported; the boy will have read Obi-Wan's two queries. At Dex's Diner Dex repeats the Kaminoan description word for word and asks why a Jedi keeps asking; Obi-Wan deflects. Night: the ridge-and-farm dream. He does not write it down.

### Ch 12
Interlude. Sidious in the Works, twenty-two days after Anakin's mind closed: the boy's mind was his garden for six years; at the last audience he found a wall, old and struck and mended, built in Sith discipline, and heard "my Master" in an apprentice's cadence. First surprise in years; he enjoys it. He has taught that discipline to two people: one fell down a shaft on Naboo, one is on Serenno. He knows of the thirty-one younglings' dream and the Bellas bout through Temple leakage (liaison Knight Aben Tuur; Vos-Kerrick's dining chain). By hologram he summons Tyranus, who denies ever seeing the boy and is believed almost entirely; ordered to the Naboo anniversary reception to look, not touch; Yoda will watch the boy on Ilum. Amidala's Rim franchise bill to be gutted in committee; the term's arithmetic; the Rim to be made unhappy via Dooku's pamphlets. A third possibility left unfinished. Dooku on Serenno before dawn: Qui-Gon, the Council, Yaddle (locked room); obedience, pride, the cost; accepts with distaste and curiosity.

### Ch 13
Anakin's POV. Obi-Wan sees the *Crucible* off at the hangar ("Bring them all home, my friend"; something edited out). Aboard, Huyang remembers Anakin's blue crystal and the two housings; Yoda gives the lesson of the crystal that calls, then looks long at Anakin: "Changed, you are. Grown," and "Grieved, you have. For whom, I do not ask." Ilum at sunrise; the waterfall runs; six children go in. Anakin counts them out: Tam (blue), Wix (green), Ilyo (blue, cut palm), Brannick (green, crying), Kessa (blue, "Is Ahsoka back?"). Five. Sunset; the door freezes. Yoda: "Her path, her own it is." Anakin goes through the closing gap; Yoda says only "Hmm." The caves test him with the lava bank, Padmé at the lake, and an ice bridge shaped like the Endor gantry; he walks through all three. He finds Tano before a wall of ice, shaking, green crystal in her fist, seeing a black shape with a red blade. He reaches into her mind and meets his own war-era shield. "Who taught you that?" "You did."

### Ch 14
Ahsoka's POV opens. Snowed into the Ilum crystal chamber till sunrise, she faces Anakin as a soldier facing an enemy in a boy's body, her green crystal her only weapon; he lays his saber on the floor untouched. She tells him Malachor and the cracked mask; he tells her Endor, Luke, the mask coming off, dying in the light. She does not believe him; he does not insist. They test each other: Mortis and the Daughter, Rex, Snips, Fives's death (the first thing she cannot check). She tells him the chips, Rex, the Venator; he already knew, and she grasps what Vader knew and withheld. He tells her Luke and Leia; Padmé's children lived; she weeps for the first time in seven years and he holds her. Rules: lie to everyone, including Obi-Wan. She would "know" if Obi-Wan were back; he tells her the Kamino/Sifo-Dyas log; she goes quiet. She says "Skyguy" once. Dawn: Yoda, "Found her, you did." "Found you, she did." Her crystal is green.

### Ch 15
Part Three opens. On the *Crucible* home, Huyang helps the six younglings build their sabers; Ahsoka fakes a child's mistakes, and her green blade igniting sends Anakin across a thousand battlefields. Obi-Wan meets them at the hangar; his look goes to Anakin, to the Tano child, then to the floor between them. Back on Coruscant they cannot be seen together, so Anakin sets a waterproof case behind the third waterfall in the Room of a Thousand Fountains. First notes fail (his Vader cipher, her Togruti); they agree Aurebesh shifted seven ("Tano, seventh of seven") and sign as S. On the nineteenth night they make a deliberate bond, thin as thread, and the shock runs both ways. Jokes about her height and his breaking voice. She asks what he did in the Temple; he tells her everything, Drallig in this very room, the eight children in the Council chamber, no excuse. She does not forgive him and works with him anyway. They inventory what each knows; the gap is Obi-Wan. They will test him later.

### Ch 16
Obi-Wan's POV. The morning after the Crucible docks, Yoda reports Ilum over tea: Anakin walked into the closing caves against custom and came out at dawn carrying the Tano child; Yoda repeats "Found you, she did" twice and Obi-Wan's hand closes on a cold cup. Over the following weeks he sees the boy and the girl everywhere (the Fountains room, corridors, the archive walk), never together, never speaking, the distance between them kept with precision. "Of all the children in the Temple, that one." Plo Koon talks of little 'Soka, last out, first to speak, "older than her years"; Obi-Wan answers with nothing true. He sets Anakin the Soresu closing circle blind and watches him fail it badly at the narrow end, then says: "You are hiding something from me. I would rather you lied to me well than badly." A boy's silence. Obi-Wan gives him the day and a speeder and does not follow. That night the desert dream: the boy at the vaporator turns round. Obi-Wan wakes with a name he does not say aloud.

### Ch 17
The invitation to the sixth Naboo anniversary reception comes through the Council; Obi-Wan tried to refuse and Windu overruled him. The night before, Anakin finds Ahsoka's dead-drop note: three rules (Dooku will touch him with the Force; the Chancellor will watch Dooku watch him; "Don't look at her like she's already dead") and a joke about poisoned drinks. Morning: formal robes with too-short sleeves, the lists moved to the belt lining, Obi-Wan re-tying the braid and straightening his collar with a hand that stays. The Dome Gallery: Gungans, Amedda, Sly Moore, Organa, Mothma, Orn Free Taa sneering at Amidala's bill. Palpatine's hand on his shoulder, the mind-touch resting on the wall as on a fence he owns; the toast to Naboo. Dooku arrives, courteous to Obi-Wan; his hand hangs a finger from Anakin's shoulder and his Force-touch goes through the surface to the wall. Anakin's hatred (the arm; the kneeling; "Do it") flares a second too late; Dooku's eyes change; Palpatine watches both, pleased. Then the doors: Padmé, twenty, in blue, Typho behind her.

### Ch 18
Padmé crosses the Dome Gallery to Anakin and Obi-Wan ("Ani? My goodness, you've grown"); Anakin answers with a Temple bow and "Senator Amidala." She says she wrote twice after Naboo, once to "Ani Skywalker" (returned) and once care of Master Kenobi (never answered); Anakin, who never knew, says the Temple is strict about letters; neither he nor Obi-Wan looks at the other. Anakin decides the only way to keep her alive is to make sure she never loves him, and plays a cold, well-mannered Padawan ("we're meant to be useful"); she registers a small hurt and a puzzle; Obi-Wan recognises what he is doing and grieves down the bond. Anakin's one true thing: never trust anyone who says the Senate needs a stronger hand. She laughs: "You sound like Captain Typho," touches his arm, and goes. On the terrace Anakin comes apart in silence; Obi-Wan stands beside him: "I know." / "You don't." / "No. I suppose I don't." Dooku watches Anakin; Palpatine watches Dooku.

### Ch 19
Interlude, Dooku's POV, the night of the reception. Leaving at the eleventh hour, Dooku replays his forbidden touch of Anakin's mind: a competent boy's surface, a wall laid in Sith grammar he recognises from his own training, and one second of hatred through it, personal, with the shape of a history (something taken, something given up on one's knees) though he has never met the boy. The boy's face never moved. He recalls watching Anakin with Amidala and with Kenobi on the terrace. In the Works, Sidious in person: "The boy is not yours, and he is not mine. Someone has had him." / "Then we will find out who." The plan: remove Kenobi the way Jedi lose masters ("It is how it made you"), test the boy in grief, bring back a recording. Dooku hires Jango (oversee, record, don't touch the Padawan), arranges the Pyke hostage-taking of Deshan Orr's eleven, and a Senator's request for Kenobi by name. Privately: he does not entirely wish to know who had the boy.

### Ch 20
Ahsoka's POV, open. She has spent three tendays training Master Plo, via Kessa's childish questions, to tell her Council business; Master Sen watches her. Anakin's drop-note about the reception (Padmé's "Ani", Dooku's touch, "one second over the top") has frightened her: she knows Dooku reads a second as a signature. She passes Barriss Offee, twelve and kind, and cannot decide whether to hate her. A water fight in the Room of a Thousand Fountains makes her laugh, until a Temple Guard appears (not the Pau'an); Sen scolds; Barriss drops her a towel. In "Jedi and Sith" the clan makes her the Sith; she dies on the grass thinking of Malachor. She catches Caleb on the worn fourth step, holds him too long; he asks why she's crying; she isn't. Plo tells her, as routine, that Kenobi goes to Oba Diah for eleven surveyors held by the Pykes, asked for by a Senator through the Chancellor's office. She knows the Pykes from Mandalore and her Fulcrum years. Dead-drop: "Oba Diah. Pykes. It's a trap. Who asked for him by name?" She waits.

### Ch 21
Anakin reads Ahsoka's dead-drop warning about Oba Diah and answers: he does not know who asked for Obi-Wan by name (the request came via the Chancellor's office), but he knows the place from Obi-Wan's telling in another life, and Obi-Wan, if returned, knows it too and is going anyway. In their quarters he asks straight why Obi-Wan searched for Kamino; Obi-Wan's Sifo-Dyas story (Qui-Gon's effects, Dooku's corridor question, an Ord Mantell docket for the Rishi Maze, an unknown word) fits every log entry and Anakin cannot tell truth from lie. Obi-Wan never asks how he got the logs. Obi-Wan insists Anakin stay, the first time in six years; Anakin's fury is Vader's and he knows it is borrowed. At the hangar Obi-Wan says he would still have Anakin at his back, "but not this time"; the phrase throws Anakin to Malachor. The courier lifts. Ahsoka, twenty metres off with a permission slip, pretends not to look.

### Ch 22
Obi-Wan lands on Oba Diah, dark and spired, met by Lom Pyke with a courtesy too perfect; his body has known since the Council chamber that this is a trap ("the Pykes. Of course it would be the Pykes"). Deshan Orr and her team, eleven in all, are held in a round tower room ringed by a cargo gantry. He negotiates brilliantly, turning the mineral-rights pretext into a case for the Amidala tribunal bill, winning release terms while knowing the floor is a trap. On the gantry a man in silver-blue Mandalorian armour watches, helmet recording; Obi-Wan knows him with impossible certainty and refuses the name. Trade Federation surplus droids and Pyke guards spring the trap; Soresu holds while Orr counts her people into the lift. A bolt takes him through the side, another in the shoulder on the gantry; he falls, thinks of a desert and a farm, says "I'm sorry, Anakin. For all of it," and in the dark someone pulls the thin bond hard enough to hurt.

### Ch 23
Anakin's POV, open. Mid-class in Drallig's hall with Bear Clan, the bond tears open: Obi-Wan's wounds come through like a blade. Anakin hauls on the thread and lies to Drallig ("cramp"). Ahsoka reaches him through their faint bond and meets him at the dead-drop: "Go." She lies to the Temple Guard (a message from Master Plo), lies to Knight Sabo Thane with a straight face, and has already sliced the hangar log so the second courier reads as a repulsor test flight. Anakin notes she is better at this than he remembers, and that it is horrible to watch. He flies as Vader, computes the jump by hand, endures hours in hyperspace fearing the thread will stop, and vows. On Oba Diah he fights as what he is: heavy, one-handed, economical; a Pyke choked off the floor; droids halved; Jango's helmet recording, then gone. He finds Obi-Wan under the stair, lifts him with the Force, frees the surveyors in passing, dresses the wounds aboard, jumps, and says "Stay with me, Ben."

### Ch 24
Obi-Wan surfaces in the courier with "Ben" hanging in the cabin; the name unlocks the withheld life: the Jundland hut, the ridge above the Lars farm, Owen and Beru, the Inquisitors, Leia, the cracked helmet, the Death Star corridor, waking at twenty-seven over tea, four years of letters, audiences, Archives and the list of the dead. He asks where Anakin heard it; "From my son." Anakin, flying and turning round, tells Bespin, the throne room, the lightning, throwing Sidious into the shaft, the mask coming off, the light. Obi-Wan tells nineteen years on the ridge, what he believed Anakin was, and why he told no one. Then Mustafar, six lines with silence between. Nothing healed; something begun. Anakin says there is someone else to meet. They drop out over Coruscant and agree what they will have to lie about.

### Ch 25
Anakin's POV. The courier lands; Sabo Thane says nothing and files it. Vokara Che wants Obi-Wan in the tank for days; he wins three hours on a stim and stubbornness. In the High Council Chamber Windu lays out four matters (stolen courier, unassigned Padawan, Mandalorian, eleven surveyors); Obi-Wan, grey and strapped, lies fluently that he summoned Anakin by private comm and takes the order on himself. Plo Koon notes the hangar-log repulsor test and declines to pursue it; Yoda watches the boy throughout; Windu does not believe and cannot break it. Anakin finds he can no longer hate Windu, who is right. At night, behind the third waterfall, Obi-Wan meets Ahsoka ("Master Kenobi." / "Ahsoka."); beard, height, Anakin's voice cracking. Three lists on the stone. Ahsoka tells the chips; Obi-Wan learns Cody did not choose and folds. Kamino, Sifo-Dyas's saber, Verrane, the wall, Dooku's touch, Jango's helmet. "So we're all lying to everyone." / "Yes. Try to do it well."
