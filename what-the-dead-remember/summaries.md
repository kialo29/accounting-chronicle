
### Ch 1
Endor: Vader's suit fails in the Death Star hangar; Luke drags him to the ramp and removes the mask. Anakin sees his son's blue eyes unfiltered, says "You were right about me. Tell your sister... you were right," and dies in white light. He wakes in the dark in a narrow Temple bed, breathing without a machine, with two flesh hands, no scars, no pain, a Padawan braid, a boy's cracking voice. He feels Obi-Wan asleep through the training bond thirty paces away and slams a Sith shield across it on reflex, then realises the slam itself betrayed him. A chrono and the pre-war skyline place him six years before Geonosis. Obi-Wan knocks, bearded, thirty-one, and asks "Nightmare?"; Anakin says "Yes, Master", his first lie, and Obi-Wan, after an oddly long look round the room, believes him. Till dawn Anakin decides: hide everything, change nothing yet, find out why he is here and whether he is alone. Far below, something rings like a struck bell.
