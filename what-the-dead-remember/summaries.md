
### Ch 1
Endor: Vader's suit fails in the Death Star hangar; Luke drags him to the ramp and removes the mask. Anakin sees his son's blue eyes unfiltered, says "You were right about me. Tell your sister... you were right," and dies in white light. He wakes in the dark in a narrow Temple bed, breathing without a machine, with two flesh hands, no scars, no pain, a Padawan braid, a boy's cracking voice. He feels Obi-Wan asleep through the training bond thirty paces away and slams a Sith shield across it on reflex, then realises the slam itself betrayed him. A chrono and the pre-war skyline place him six years before Geonosis. Obi-Wan knocks, bearded, thirty-one, and asks "Nightmare?"; Anakin says "Yes, Master", his first lie, and Obi-Wan, after an oddly long look round the room, believes him. Till dawn Anakin decides: hide everything, change nothing yet, find out why he is here and whether he is alone. Far below, something rings like a struck bell.

### Ch 2
First morning. Obi-Wan is wrong: he smiles too fast, teases, puts a hand on Anakin's shoulder and calls him "my friend"; Anakin cannot tell whether his memory has rotted or the man has changed, and resolves to write it down. Obi-Wan reminds him he assists Drallig with Hawkbat Clan's saber class every tenday. Anakin walks into a hall of twenty loud, bored children and functions, correcting grips and kneeling to a Rodian girl's height, while Order 66 cuts in: the 501st on the steps, the Council chamber, Drallig's eleven-second death, the boy's question. In the back corner a small Togruta initiate holds her training saber reversed; Drallig calls her "Tano" and Anakin's knees nearly go. He kneels, takes her wrist, his hand shakes; she watches the hand, not his face, says one word. He lets go without correcting the grip; she turns it back round. Afterwards he retches in a refresher: she is ten, she is a child.

### Ch 3
Same day, Ahsoka Tano. Awake since the struck-bell note in the night, she lies in her Hawkbat bunk, feet not reaching the mattress end, naming nothing. She performs ten: lets Kessa Ruun fix her lek bead, wears a tunic a quarter too small, steers Caleb Dume round a pillar and holds on a half-second too long, cannot look at Barriss. Master Plo finds "little 'Soka"; she asks whether he felt anything in the night (he did not) and he recalls her asking at five whether a dream can be true. In saber class the Padawan arrives late and white, counts the ranks, kneels to Kessa with the wrong smile; his hand shakes on her wrist, studies her reversed grip, asks "Always?", she says "Yes", he lets it be; she reverses it again. She adds an unseen eighth line to her Togruti list. At night she practises two reversed sabers in a dim dojo, feels watched from the empty gallery, senses something new and hard-shielded in the Temple. Last line: something came in that night wearing Skywalker's face.

### Ch 4
Nine days after waking, Anakin keeps his standing tenth-day audience with the Chancellor. Obi-Wan walks him to the Senate Office Building, lingers at the foot of the steps and visibly cannot go up; Anakin recalls his failed Council request to end the audiences. In the red office Palpatine pours muja juice and talks Outer Rim trade, Amidala's reform bill with Organa and Mothma, Dooku's privately printed pamphlet on Senate ethics, and his own approaching term limit, while his mind-touch strolls the boy's mind. Anakin feeds it a built surface (Drallig's forms, a Mobquet speeder, a generic mother-hollow, an invented crush on Padawan Mirren Dask) and lets a boy's flush cover what Padmé's name does to him. The touch reaches the Sith-built wall, stops, and is pleased. Anakin says "my Master" in Vader's kneeling cadence; Palpatine's eyes hold on him. Walking home: the sense of a door tried; a flash of Endor lightning and the Emperor's weight.

### Ch 5
Obi-Wan's morning, day ten: tea, the Temple's shadow on the skyline, and his private "accounts" of small changes: the letters (three years), the initiate classes (two), never a whole day with the Chancellor, and yesterday's failure to climb the Senate steps. The bond closed ten nights ago "like a door"; he has told no one. Yoda summons him to the eastern spire: a shadow, new and close, the dark side's stain in the house; the Council will look. Obi-Wan deflects without lying outright ("I would not call what I have felt in him that"); Yoda notes he felt Obi-Wan's door close too. In Drallig's hall Anakin loses to Bellas on purpose; one one-handed parry stops Obi-Wan's breath; Drallig says he never taught it. Alone below the Council chamber, a green convor sits on the ledge; something old and bright makes his eyes sting. "Not again. Whatever this is. Not again."

### Ch 6
Afternoon of day ten. Obi-Wan hands Anakin a courier letter "as if it were ordinary": from Shmi, free, married to Cliegg Lars, stepson Owen sixteen, the vaporators, the fence; she keeps his last letter in her apron. In Anakin's memory there were no letters. He opens the courier archive and reads three of his own boy-letters (twelve, thirteen, fourteen) and cannot bear the handwriting. A flash of the Tusken tent interrupts and resolves nothing. He writes back as a boy, tearing off three sheets before one holds. He starts the datapad list in Vader's cipher (keyed to the first respirator's serial; nobody alive can read it): letters, Obi-Wan's warmth, the Tano child's grip, the bell; three theories. In the Archives he tests memory against record: Carnelion IV matches; his own file matches. Jocasta Nu passes. Idly he enters "Kamino": no record, but one prior unfilled query, KENOBI, O., two years ago. Every rationalisation fails. He does not add it to the list. He adds it.

### Ch 7
Night of day ten: exhausted, Anakin sleeps unshielded. Full Order 66 dream: the wet Processional Way, the 501st's boots behind him, Jurokk's question at the gate, the Temple Guard, Drallig killed in the dark Room of a Thousand Fountains, the Council tower stairs, Bear Clan younglings hiding among the chairs, a boy glad to see him, the blue blade. He wakes to the Temple ringing with crying children (Hawkbat and Bear among them), feels Yoda sit up in the dark, and knows he did this, twice. Obi-Wan sits on his bed, hand on his shin. Morning: Halls of Healing. Vokara Che reports thirty-one younglings shared one dream centred near his level, asks to walk it; he forges a Tatooine dream (sand, his mother's hands, a slaver's whip) in four seconds. She finds it, then the wall, and tells Obi-Wan in front of him: "a wall, old and scarred, as if built over years"; "Who taught you to shield?" "No. He didn't." Windu in the corridor: "now is the time." Anakin lies. Obi-Wan never asks about the wall.

### Ch 8
Ahsoka POV, night of day ten/eleven through day eighteen. She is inside the shared dream: a stair she knows, a burning hall, ranks of white she will not name, a boy's face at the end of a blue blade asking a question. She wakes on the floor between bunks; all sixteen Hawkbat children cry; she does not, is sick silently in the refresher, then holds Kessa. Bear Clan is brought in to be counted (thirty-one); Caleb Dume grips her sleeve. Plo comes; she asks whether the Council would believe a youngling whose dream was true; he gives a Master's answer, recalls her asking at five (she lies: "I don't remember"), looks at her a long time. Master Sen announces Kenobi's Padawan will accompany Yoda's Gathering; Ahsoka manoeuvres (Plo, Sen, Kessa as cover) onto the list, seventh of seven. At the Fountains Kenobi looks at her a beat too long; she looks away first. Night practice: dust on the gallery rail worn where someone sat.

### Ch 9
Anakin's POV. In Master Vos-Kerrick's politics class the holonet shows Count Dooku, 76, in black at a Serenno institute, delivering a seductive indictment of Senate corruption ("patience is a sentence"). Bellas, Mirren Dask and the others argue; Anakin stays silent, flashes to the Invisible Hand (Dooku kneeling, two blades, Palpatine's "Do it"), then answers as a boy. In Drallig's advanced class he throws two bouts to Orrin Bellas, 17, who calls him "slave boy" and says Kenobi only kept him for a dying promise to Jinn; instinct takes over: a one-handed overhand sends Bellas's saber the length of the hall and stops a finger from his throat. Drallig: "Where did you learn that?" "I don't know, Master." Obi-Wan in the doorway; the thin bond carries something that is not surprise. Drallig sets morning sessions until the Crucible leaves; Bellas gets Bear Clan. At night on the Temple roof a green convor sits three metres off, watching, and does not fly. "Not Palpatine. Something older." End of Part One.

### Ch 10
Part Two opens on day twenty. After Drallig's dawn penance Anakin goes to the Archives. Jocasta Nu, courteous and exact, grants him his master's training-materials access record and refuses the general query log. At terminal nine, beneath the Soresu commentaries, Vader's hands slice the Archive's bones through a nine-hundred-year-old maintenance account in four minutes, and it frightens him. He finds Obi-Wan's full Kamino record (28 BBY, terminal four, forty minutes, null) and, four days later, "Sifo-Dyas", filled, read for eleven minutes. He tries an innocent story; the four days break it. Unable to stop, he opens Initiate Tano's file: healer's note, 31 BBY, age five, night terrors, "whether a dream can be a memory", no recurrence. He refuses the conclusion. On a spire landing he writes a four-line theory board (Obi-Wan / the child / both / neither) and hates each. At supper he drops "the Duchess of Mandalore"; Obi-Wan, mild: "Who told you about Satine?" Unreadable. Anakin realises he enjoyed the game and is ashamed. Ilum in three days.
