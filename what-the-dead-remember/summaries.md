
### Ch 1
Endor: Vader's suit fails in the Death Star hangar; Luke drags him to the ramp and removes the mask. Anakin sees his son's blue eyes unfiltered, says "You were right about me. Tell your sister... you were right," and dies in white light. He wakes in the dark in a narrow Temple bed, breathing without a machine, with two flesh hands, no scars, no pain, a Padawan braid, a boy's cracking voice. He feels Obi-Wan asleep through the training bond thirty paces away and slams a Sith shield across it on reflex, then realises the slam itself betrayed him. A chrono and the pre-war skyline place him six years before Geonosis. Obi-Wan knocks, bearded, thirty-one, and asks "Nightmare?"; Anakin says "Yes, Master", his first lie, and Obi-Wan, after an oddly long look round the room, believes him. Till dawn Anakin decides: hide everything, change nothing yet, find out why he is here and whether he is alone. Far below, something rings like a struck bell.

### Ch 2
First morning. Obi-Wan is wrong: he smiles too fast, teases, puts a hand on Anakin's shoulder and calls him "my friend"; Anakin cannot tell whether his memory has rotted or the man has changed, and resolves to write it down. Obi-Wan reminds him he assists Drallig with Hawkbat Clan's saber class every tenday. Anakin walks into a hall of twenty loud, bored children and functions, correcting grips and kneeling to a Rodian girl's height, while Order 66 cuts in: the 501st on the steps, the Council chamber, Drallig's eleven-second death, the boy's question. In the back corner a small Togruta initiate holds her training saber reversed; Drallig calls her "Tano" and Anakin's knees nearly go. He kneels, takes her wrist, his hand shakes; she watches the hand, not his face, says one word. He lets go without correcting the grip; she turns it back round. Afterwards he retches in a refresher: she is ten, she is a child.

### Ch 3
Same day, Ahsoka Tano. Awake since the struck-bell note in the night, she lies in her Hawkbat bunk, feet not reaching the mattress end, naming nothing. She performs ten: lets Kessa Ruun fix her lek bead, wears a tunic a quarter too small, steers Caleb Dume round a pillar and holds on a half-second too long, cannot look at Barriss. Master Plo finds "little 'Soka"; she asks whether he felt anything in the night (he did not) and he recalls her asking at five whether a dream can be true. In saber class the Padawan arrives late and white, counts the ranks, kneels to Kessa with the wrong smile; his hand shakes on her wrist, studies her reversed grip, asks "Always?", she says "Yes", he lets it be; she reverses it again. She adds an unseen eighth line to her Togruti list. At night she practises two reversed sabers in a dim dojo, feels watched from the empty gallery, senses something new and hard-shielded in the Temple. Last line: something came in that night wearing Skywalker's face.
