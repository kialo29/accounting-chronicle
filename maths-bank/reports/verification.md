## How the bank was verified

1. **Schema and integrity validation** (`node scripts/validate.mjs`): required fields and types, unique ids, every prerequisite / linked topic / misconception id exists, prerequisite graph is acyclic, `$…$`/`$$…$$` delimiters balance, every maths span parses in KaTeX, no LaTeX outside maths, no JSON escape damage, SVG diagrams are well-formed and safe, markdown tables are rectangular, and every topic has 15–20 questions, all 5 difficulties, ≥4 question types, ≥2 synoptic questions, ≥1 exam-style question, a variation-theory sequence and every misconception targeted.
2. **Independent blind checking**: a separate checker agent (never the writer) printed each topic's questions without hints/answers (`scripts/blind.mjs`), solved every question itself (with sympy/scipy for numerical and statistical work), and only then compared with the stored answer and worked solution. Mismatches were fixed; ambiguous questions were reworded so there is exactly one intended answer. The checker also verified each topic's worked examples and misconceptions and rendered every diagram to PNG to look at it.
3. **Diagram audit**: a third pass by separate agents rendered every SVG diagram and checked it against the question text and answer (values, scales, grid alignment, proportions, legibility, no give-aways).
4. **Browser smoke test** (`node scripts/smoke-test.mjs`): loads the app in headless Chromium at desktop and phone widths in light and dark mode, exercises search, filters, topic pages, reveals, learning paths and the worksheet builder, prints worksheet and answer-sheet PDFs, and renders every formula in the bank through KaTeX in the browser.
