# The Wrong Verdict — Outline (Book One)

Target: 30 chapters, ~3,800 words each (epilogue ~2,500). Total ~110k.

## PART ONE: THE VERDICT

### Ch01 — The Masked Jedi (POV: Anakin)
Divergence point. Opens the night after Ahsoka's canon recapture in the nano-droid warehouse. Tarkin has demanded the Council expel her so a military tribunal can try her; the Council answers at dawn. Anakin goes into the lower levels alone to find Ventress, the only witness. He finds her, bruised and furious: her lightsabers were stolen by the masked attacker. Before she can tell him what she knows (including that Ahsoka contacted a friend in the Temple), the masked figure strikes from above with Ventress's own red sabers to silence her. A three-way fight. Ventress, unarmed, is driven over a ledge and vanishes. Anakin pursues the masked figure, a skilled duelist who fights like Temple-trained. She detonates nano-droid charges on a support pylon, and a crowded lower-level transit platform and hab section begin to collapse. Anakin chooses to hold it with the Force and get people clear. The figure escapes and Ventress is gone. Commander Fox's Coruscant Guard arrive. Anakin's account (a masked figure with Ventress's red sabers) is recorded skeptically: Ventress's accomplice, surely. Final short scene, POV switch: in an alley, the figure removes her mask. It is Barriss Offee, shaking, with Ventress's sabers in her hands. She cannot make herself throw them away.

### Ch02 — Expelled (POV: Ahsoka)
Ahsoka in a cell at the Republic military base (Republic Center for Military Operations), cuffed. Her lightsabers are now evidence in the deaths of the three Coruscant Guard clones killed outside her cell during her "escape". She knows she didn't kill them, and someone used her blades to make it look as if she had. Anakin's failed chase is reported to her. The Jedi Council hearing: she is expelled from the Order so she can be tried by a Republic military tribunal. Plo Koon cannot meet her eyes; Anakin argues and loses. She removes nothing herself — her Padawan beads are taken. Padmé agrees to defend her. Anakin visits her cell: she tells him Ventress is the only witness, and that the masked attacker fought like someone trained in the Temple. He swears he'll find Ventress. Alone, Ahsoka realizes the Order she gave her life to has let her go without a fight. End: news that the prosecutor will be Admiral Wilhuff Tarkin, and that he will seek the death penalty.

### Ch03 — The Healer (POV: Barriss)
Barriss inside the Temple after the bombing, among memorial candles for the dead. Her ideology: the Jedi have become soldiers; the bombing was meant to wake them. Her guilt is physical — insomnia, shaking hands, a bruise from the fight with Anakin she hides from Luminara. She hides Ventress's sabers in a sealed, disused maintenance crawlway deep beneath the Temple instead of destroying them — the one thing that could clear Ahsoka; she can't explain to herself why she keeps them. She visits Ahsoka in detention as a friend before the trial; Ahsoka's gratitude cuts her. She assumed Ahsoka would only be expelled; Tarkin's call for death horrifies her. She nearly confesses to Luminara, and doesn't.

### Ch04 — The Tribunal (POV: Padmé)
The military tribunal in the Senate-side chamber, Chancellor Palpatine presiding with apparent fairness. Tarkin's case is clinical. It covers the security footage of Letta Turmond choking while alone with Ahsoka, and the three clones cut down with Ahsoka's own lightsabers. It covers her escape, her alliance with Ventress, her capture amid the nano-droid stockpile, and Anakin's own testimony about a figure with red lightsabers, which Tarkin reads as Ventress or Ventress's accomplice. Padmé fights hard: no motive, no proof, a model record. Ahsoka testifies with dignity and some anger. Padmé asks who told her where the warehouse was. Ahsoka refuses to name the friend, because naming her would expel that friend for aiding a fugitive. The refusal hurts her badly. (Barriss sits in the gallery; Ahsoka's eyes go to her once.) Padmé sees how the room has already decided: a frightened Senate wants someone to blame. Padmé and Anakin argue privately about him going rogue to find Ventress. Ends with closing arguments done and the tribunal retiring.

### Ch05 — The Chancellor's Mercy (POV: Anakin; short Palpatine/Sidious coda)
Anakin's desperate night hunt for Ventress in the underworld fails. Verdict: guilty on all counts. Tarkin asks for execution. During the recess Anakin goes to Palpatine's office and begs. Palpatine is gentle, regretful, makes no promise. At sentencing Palpatine pronounces death — then, invoking wartime clemency and her service, commutes it to life imprisonment at the Republic penal facility on Narkis. Ahsoka's face when she hears "life." Anakin's overwhelming gratitude to Palpatine; his fury at the Council, who did nothing. Short coda from Palpatine's POV (never named as Sidious in-scene, but the reader understands): satisfaction at the debt Anakin now owes.

### Ch06 — Good Soldiers (POV: Rex)
The 501st barracks after the verdict: Fives furious, Jesse bitter, Kix quiet, Tup with a headache he shrugs off (foreshadowing). Rex keeps discipline and is privately breaking. Using Anakin's pull, Rex visits Ahsoka before the transfer. She is composed for his sake; she gives him one last "order": look after Anakin, he's not as strong as he thinks. Rex can't salute her because she isn't his commander anymore; he does anyway. Rex's dawning conflict: the Republic he serves did this, and he followed orders that helped. He watches the prison transport lift off.

### Ch07 — The Deep (POV: Ahsoka)
Transport to Narkis, an ice moon at the edge of Wild Space. The Force-dampening collar: the Force is gone for the first time in her life — a deafness, a grief. Processing by the Warden, Commandant Ilsa Vorne (correct, unbending, a Tarkin protégé). The prison carved into the ice: "the Deep." Inmates who hate Jedi; a Trandoshan named Krasso who knows she killed his kinsman Garnac on Wasskah. An older human smuggler, Tess Varro, gives cynical advice. First night: cold, despair, nightmares of the hangar explosion. Ends with Ahsoka choosing to survive — and noticing a Separatist prisoner held in isolation.

## PART TWO: THE DEEP

### Ch08 — The Weight of Gratitude (POV: Anakin)
Six weeks later. Anakin is reckless on the front lines (a brutal Outer Rim battle). Obi-Wan's concern is met with coldness. Anakin's secret investigation: the nano-droids' black-market source, a dealer in the lower levels — found dead before Anakin reaches him, scene too clean. Someone is cleaning up. Padmé's appeal is denied. Palpatine dangles hope: when the war is won, a Chancellor might grant pardons the Council would never allow. Anakin's resentment of the Council hardens. Ends with Anakin granted a visitation permit to Narkis, signed by the Chancellor.

### Ch09 — Garnac's Blood (POV: Ahsoka)
Prison life in the ice mines and refinery. Tess teaches her the rules. Krasso tries to kill her in the mine; without the Force Ahsoka wins through training, speed and wits, and spares him. The guards punish her with solitary. In isolation, through a vent, she meets the Separatist prisoner Merrick Tal, a captured CIS officer from Raxus — an idealist who argues the Republic that caged her is the real enemy. She disagrees, but his words stick. She begins to meditate without the Force, finding a different kind of stillness.

### Ch10 — The Chancellor's Friend (POV: Barriss)
Palpatine requests a Jedi healer for "a staff matter"; Barriss is sent. Alone in his office, he reveals he has sealed the security recordings from the night Letta Turmond died. He is understanding, sorrowful — he too fears what the war has made of the Jedi — and he will keep her secret. He asks only friendship: tell him what the Council fears. Barriss tells herself the Senate should oversee the Jedi. She agrees. The room feels cold. Luminara notices Barriss changing. Barriss volunteers for healer rotations at Republic prisons — including Narkis — as penance she can't name.

### Ch11 — Visitors (POV: Ahsoka)
Anakin visits Narkis: a raw, guarded reunion through restraints. He's thinner, angrier; he talks of the Chancellor's promised pardon and of the Council with contempt. She sees something in him that frightens her and makes him promise not to do anything stupid. Weeks later, injured in a mine accident, she is treated by the visiting Jedi healer: Barriss. Dramatic irony at full strength: Ahsoka's gratitude, a shared memory of Geonosis, Barriss asking whether she hates the Council. Ahsoka: she's trying not to. Barriss leaves shaking. Ahsoka notes it as kindness.

### Ch12 — Ringo Vinda (POV: Rex)
The battle of Ringo Vinda. Tup suddenly kills Jedi General Tiplar, repeating "good soldiers follow orders." Chaos, grief. Fives insists something is wrong with Tup and accompanies him to Kamino. Rex, watching another trusted person branded a traitor overnight, feels the same sickness he felt at Ahsoka's verdict. Before leaving, Fives tells Rex: if anything happens, he won't go quietly the way she had to. Rex begins quietly keeping records of things that don't add up.

### Ch13 — What Fives Found (POV: Fives)
Kamino: Tup's death, the hidden inhibitor chip, Fives removing his own chip with the medical droid's help, Nala Se and Lama Su's cover-up. Before going to Coruscant, Fives sends Rex an encrypted burst — because Ahsoka's trial taught him what happens to the accused who trust the system. Coruscant: the meeting with the Chancellor, the setup, the flight to the warehouse district. Anakin and Rex find him; he tries to make them understand. Commander Fox's men open fire. Divergence: Rex lunges, Fox's shot hits Fives in the shoulder rather than the heart, and Fives falls through a grating into the depths of the lower levels. Cliffhanger: Fox reports the fugitive dead, body unrecoverable.

### Ch14 — Narrow Paths (POV: Anakin; Dooku scene)
Anakin, numb, accepts the official "virus" explanation; Palpatine's sympathy is perfect, and he lets slip how painful it is "when the loyal turn out to be false" — then apologizes for the comparison. Anakin snaps at Obi-Wan, who admits privately he no longer believes Ahsoka did it — which only makes Anakin angrier that he said nothing at the trial. Scene with Padmé: his nightmare of Ahsoka dying in ice. Second scene: Count Dooku on Serenno watching Holonet coverage of the convicted Jedi; a disciplined, betrayed young Force-user would make an ideal secret apprentice — one his Master need not know about. He contacts Cad Bane.

### Ch15 — The Admiral's Visit (POV: Ahsoka)
A coolant rupture and ice collapse in the mine. Vorne orders Ahsoka's collar released for minutes to help; the Force floods back and she holds the ice while Tess, guards and even Krasso get out. Collar back on; the loss hurts more than before. Tarkin arrives for an inspection. Private exchange: he tells her, coldly, that her guilt was never the point — the Republic needed an answer and she was the most useful one. Ahsoka realizes she is a symbol, not a person, to the people who caged her. Merrick Tal is told he'll be transferred. Tess notices strange ships on the scanners.

### Ch16 — Bane (POV: Ahsoka)
Night raid. Cad Bane and his crew breach Narkis for Ahsoka (and Merrick Tal). Guards die; the prison erupts. Ahsoka, collared, is taken; Tess gets aboard in the chaos. On Bane's ship Count Dooku appears by hologram: the Republic condemned you, the Jedi abandoned you — join me. She refuses. Bane says he'll deliver her either way. With Tess, Ahsoka sabotages the ship and they escape in a pod that crash-lands on Florrum. Final beat: a Holonet bulletin — "Convicted Temple Bomber Freed in Separatist Raid; Believed to Have Joined Count Dooku."

## PART THREE: THE HUNT

### Ch17 — Fugitive (POV: Anakin)
The Council meets. Mace Windu: she is presumed to have joined Dooku; she must be recaptured, by force if necessary. Anakin erupts. Obi-Wan and Anakin are assigned to find her, with the 501st. Barriss and Luminara are attached to the search (Palpatine's office recommended it). Tarkin dispatches his own team. Palpatine in private: he could not prevent the Council's authorization of lethal force; find her first, Anakin, and bring her to me. Anakin's resolve. Rex receives a coded message he recognizes as Fives's handwork: alive.

### Ch18 — Pirates (POV: Ahsoka)
Florrum. Hondo Ohnaka's pirates find the crash; Hondo is delighted to see her and openly weighs what she's worth to every side. Comic menace, real warmth. His mechanic cuts off the collar: the Force returns like sunlight; she weeps. Hondo, a collector, lends her a lightsaber from his trophy collection (a blue blade whose original owner he lies about). Tess grows quiet, calculating. Ends with a cloaked figure walking into Hondo's base: Asajj Ventress, who heard Ahsoka was for sale.

### Ch19 — Old Wounds (POV: Ventress)
Ventress's months since the warehouse: hunting whoever stole her sabers and made a fool of her. What she knows: the attacker felt like a Jedi — calm cut with rot. A lower-level nano-droid dealer described the buyer as a hooded woman with green hands and patterned skin; the dealer was killed before Ventress could return. Ventress needs a way into the Temple; Ahsoka needs a witness. A pact. Tess sells Ahsoka's location to Cad Bane; Bane attacks Florrum; Ventress, Ahsoka and Hondo's pirates fight him off. Tess, wounded, admits it and is left behind with her credits and her shame. Ahsoka sends a coded signal to Rex.

### Ch20 — The Dead Man (POV: Rex)
On the hunt with Anakin, Rex answers Fives's signal and meets him secretly in the Coruscant lower levels, thin, feverish, alive. Fives explains everything: the chips, the order that is coming, the cover-up. Rex is shaken to the core but believes him, because he has learned what disbelief costs. Rex also has Ahsoka's signal. Decision: Fives goes to her — she's the only one who'll believe him, and she needs someone at her back. Rex will cover both of them from inside the army he still serves. He arranges Fives's passage.

### Ch21 — Green Hands (POV: Ahsoka)
Ahsoka, Ventress and (arriving) Fives in a hideout. Fives's story of the chips stuns Ahsoka; Ventress mocks, then goes quiet. Ahsoka works the case with the Force back in her: who knew the warehouse location, who visited her cell before her escape, who encouraged her to run, who is a gifted duelist with Temple access — and a Mirialan. The realization that it was Barriss is devastating, and she resists it until she can't. She is certain, and has no proof but Ventress's word and a dead dealer. Ventress senses her sabers when Ahsoka mentions the Temple's depths.

### Ch22 — The Canyon (POV: Anakin)
Tracking via Tarkin's intelligence and his own bond, Anakin finds Ahsoka on Florrum's outskirts. Obi-Wan engages Ventress (dry, old enmity). Anakin and Ahsoka face each other alone in a canyon. He begs her to come back — the Chancellor will pardon her. She says the Chancellor already made his choice once. She knows who did it but won't tell him; she's seen what's growing in him and fears what he'd do. Their lightsabers never ignite. He lets her go and lies to Obi-Wan; Obi-Wan knows he's lying and lets it stand.

### Ch23 — The Order (POV: Barriss)
Palpatine contacts Barriss privately: if Tano proves her innocence, she proves Barriss's guilt; he can only protect Barriss if there is no second trial. He never says "kill her." Barriss understands. Luminara confronts her gently, directly: is there something you need to tell me? Barriss lies; Luminara knows she's lying and lets her go — the worst thing she could do. Barriss retrieves Ventress's sabers from the crawlway. A message arrives on a channel only Ahsoka knows: "I know. Meet me where you left me."

### Ch24 — Senator Amidala's Guests (POV: Padmé)
Ahsoka, Ventress and Fives reach Coruscant; Padmé hides them, risking everything. She recognizes Fives — supposedly dead — and the scale of what's happening frightens her. Ahsoka asks her not to tell Anakin; Padmé, torn between husband and conscience, agrees and hates it. Plan: draw Barriss out and record a confession before witnesses (Fives's helmet recorder; Padmé as a Senator of standing). Padmé notices how carefully Ahsoka speaks of Anakin. That night Anakin comes home; Padmé lies to him for the first time about something that matters.

### Ch25 — Where It Began (POV: Ahsoka)
The abandoned warehouse in the lower levels. Barriss arrives masked, with Ventress's sabers, and removes the mask. The conversation: the truth, Barriss's reasons, the grief, Ahsoka's fury held in check. Barriss reveals she was sent to make sure Ahsoka never testifies. The duel: Ahsoka with Hondo's blue blade against Barriss's red borrowed sabers, through the ruin where it started. Ahsoka wins. Barriss, disarmed, asks her to end it. Ahsoka refuses: she won't become the thing they convicted.

### Ch26 — The Dark Between Stars (POV: Anakin)
Anakin sensed Ahsoka on Coruscant and followed Padmé. He arrives to see Barriss on her knees and understands everything at once. Rage: he Force-chokes Barriss, lifting her — the same death Letta died. Ahsoka steps between them and reaches him: this is how it started. He lets go. The Coruscant Guard arrives under orders to fire on the fugitive; Fives must vanish before Fox sees him. Barriss stands and confesses aloud in front of Fox, Obi-Wan and Padmé, while the helmet recording runs. Ventress takes her sabers and slips away past Obi-Wan, who lets her. Ahsoka surrenders herself to Anakin.

### Ch27 — The Second Tribunal (POV: Padmé)
The tribunal reconvenes. Barriss's public confession, in her own words, an indictment of the war and the Jedi as much as of herself. Tarkin, cornered, pivots to charges for Ahsoka's escape and the Narkis dead. Palpatine, magnanimous, grants Ahsoka a full pardon and praises Anakin's loyalty publicly. The Senate applauds. Padmé watches Anakin glow with gratitude and Ahsoka stay perfectly still. Tarkin's quiet exchange with Palpatine afterward suggests he will be rewarded, not punished.

### Ch28 — Mirror (POV: Ahsoka)
Ahsoka visits Barriss in the same kind of cell Ahsoka once occupied. Honest, painful conversation; no forgiveness yet, but something. Barriss tells her the one thing she left out of the confession: the Chancellor knew from the beginning, and protected her to use her. Ahsoka doesn't know whether to believe it. Next morning Barriss is found dead — strangled through the Force. Records from the detention level show eleven missing minutes. Anakin was logged in the building that night (collecting Ahsoka). The Council's glances at him. Ahsoka asks him directly; he swears he didn't. Palpatine personally closes the inquiry "to protect a hero of the Republic." Ahsoka believes Anakin, and is more afraid than ever.

### Ch29 — The Steps (POV: Ahsoka, with a Rex scene)
The Council offers reinstatement and Knighthood. Plo Koon apologizes. Ahsoka refuses: not from bitterness, but because she now knows things the Order cannot hear yet, and must find out for herself. Rex scene: he asks Kix to remove his chip; Kix, scared, agrees. Ventress's brief farewell (a message, her style). Anakin returns Ahsoka's original lightsabers from evidence. On the Temple steps she warns him to be careful of the Chancellor; he can't hear it, but he doesn't let go of her hand. She leaves Coruscant with Fives, toward the truth about the chips. Ending: free, unjailed, unexiled by choice, and certain the galaxy is darker than anyone in the Temple believes.

### Ch30 — Epilogue: The Long Game (POV: Palpatine/Sidious)
Sidious reflects: the healer was a loose end and her death cast a useful shadow on Skywalker that will widen the gulf between him and the Council; the boy's gratitude for the pardon is a chain he forged himself. Dooku's unauthorized Bane gambit is noted and will be punished. The Togruta girl is a variable worth watching, and the clone listed dead is not. A report that Senator Amidala visited a discreet medical clinic. Final chilling line.
