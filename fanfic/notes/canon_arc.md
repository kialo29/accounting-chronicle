## 1. Plot in chronological order

### 5x17 Sabotage
- A bomb detonates in the Jedi Temple hangar (Coruscant). Wookieepedia article: "Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar". [CONFIRMED]
- Casualties: six Jedi dead, including Jedi Knight Tutso Mara, plus several clone troopers and maintenance/hangar workers. [CONFIRMED for 6 Jedi and Mara] One fan wiki (Shaniverse, non-canon) says 8 clones, 12 workers, 6 Jedi, but that is uncorroborated. Wookieepedia's exact clone/worker figures [UNSURE]. Dozens wounded, including Jarik Shan (lost an arm, per one wiki) [UNSURE].
- The Jedi Council tasks Anakin and Ahsoka with the investigation. [MEMORY]
- A Pantoran hangar worker survivor points toward Jackar Bowmani as responsible. [CONFIRMED]
- Jackar Bowmani: hangar maintenance worker / munitions expert at the Temple. He is the human bomb: his remains were riddled with nano-droids. A forensic/investigation droid (digest named it "Russo-ISC" [UNSURE]) finds Bowmani's hand and the nano-droids. Anakin and Ahsoka first suspect him of being a willing suicide bomber. [CONFIRMED]
- Nano-droids: explosive "reconstitutor" nano-droids (digest says "NM-K reconstitutor nano-droids"). They are ingested/injected, then converge and detonate when triggered (remote trigger suspected). Source of the nano-droids: a Republic-era munitions/warehouse supply, later traced to an abandoned munitions factory/warehouse in the lower levels (see 5x19). [CONFIRMED in outline; trigger mechanism [UNSURE]]
- Anakin and Ahsoka search the couple's apartment and learn Letta Turmond fed Jackar the nano-droids (without him knowing, per digest). Letta Turmond: Bowmani's wife, an anti-war Coruscanti who stepped forward from the protesters outside the Temple and identified herself as his wife. She is arrested. [CONFIRMED]
- Barriss Offee supplied/taught Letta how to use the nano-droids (revealed only in 5x20). [CONFIRMED]
- Letta is interrogated (Anakin/Ahsoka). She withholds the mastermind. [MEMORY: she is transferred to Republic military custody because clones died]

### 5x18 The Jedi Who Knew Too Much
- Admiral Tarkin takes Letta to a Republic military prison because clones were killed in the bombing. [CONFIRMED] Location: the "Republic military base" / "Republic Center for Military Operations" on Coruscant (Wookieepedia: "Republic military base"; event "Escape from the Republic military base"). [CONFIRMED by digests; exact in-universe facility name [UNSURE]]

- Security footage: appears to show Ahsoka alone with Letta, Letta choking, no audio (Tarkin points to the lack of audio / the images as incriminating). Ahsoka looks like the only possible suspect. [CONFIRMED that footage appears to show Ahsoka choking her; exact audio detail [UNSURE]]

Barriss infiltrated the base and used Ahsoka's own confiscated lightsabers to cut down Coruscant Guard clones outside the cells, then left the keycard. Ahsoka escapes and finds most guards unconscious; Fox finds her standing among three dead clones, believes she killed them. [CONFIRMED]
- Pursuit: Fox, Anakin and Captain Rex join the chase. Anakin calls to her. She flees the complex into the Coruscant lower levels. [CONFIRMED/MEMORY mix]

### 5x19 To Catch a Jedi
- Ahsoka hides in the "deepest levels of the Coruscant underworld". [CONFIRMED]
- Asajj Ventress finds her (Ventress was independently in the lower levels, first suspects a setup). They ally: Ventress helps in exchange for a pardon / Council deal. [CONFIRMED]
- Clue trail: Ahsoka contacts Barriss, who feeds her information that the saboteur got the explosives from a warehouse not far from where Ahsoka is. [CONFIRMED] Warehouse described as an old munitions factory/warehouse, one source says "level 1315" [UNSURE; not 1313 as in the task brief].
- At the warehouse (crates of nano-droids, the same type as at the Temple), Ahsoka and Ventress split up. Ventress is ambushed and knocked out by a masked figure. The masked figure wears Ventress-style armor and mask, wields Ventress's two curved-hilt lightsabers, and attacks Ahsoka. Ahsoka is subdued/recaptured amid the nano-droids. [CONFIRMED]
- How Barriss got the sabers: she attacked Ventress earlier, stole her lightsabers (Ventress later tells Anakin this). Barriss summons Ventress's lightsabers to her hands when revealed in 5x20. [CONFIRMED]
- Anakin has been tracking Ahsoka; his report of her with Ventress worsens her case. Ahsoka is returned to the Temple/custody. [CONFIRMED]

### 5x20 The Wrong Jedi
- Jedi Council decision: Admiral Tarkin, speaking for the Senate/Republic, demands Ahsoka be expelled so she can face a Republic military tribunal (the Jedi would look biased; clones died). Obi-Wan urges the Council to refuse and stand with her. Council members cite the strong circumstantial evidence; Mace Windu notes protecting her could look like opposition to the Senate. The Council reluctantly expels her, making her a civilian under Republic jurisdiction. Anakin is furious. [CONFIRMED]
- Tribunal: held in the Republic Center for Military Operations. Presided by Supreme Chancellor Palpatine. Prosecutor: Admiral Wilhuff Tarkin, demanding the death penalty. Defense: Senator Padme Amidala. Jury of senators including Riyo Chuchi, Mon Mothma, Bail Organa, Mot-Not Rab, Kin Robb. [CONFIRMED]
- Padme argues the circumstances of Letta's death imply Ahsoka could not be the culprit. [CONFIRMED]
- Anakin investigates: Ventress tells him Ahsoka contacted Barriss and that her own lightsabers were stolen; Anakin deduces Barriss. Anakin takes a lightsaber from Barriss's quarters/confronts her at the Temple. [CONFIRMED in outline]
- Barriss, with Ventress's lightsabers, duels Anakin through the Temple (Temple Guards intervene), breaks out via a window, leaps to the courtyard, flees to the Temple training ground. Anakin overpowers and disarms her; Temple Guards arrest her. [CONFIRMED]
- Anakin and Temple Guards march Barriss into the tribunal just as the jury is about to convict (digest: majority guilty of sedition [UNSURE]). Barriss confesses.
- Barriss's motive: the Jedi have been corrupted by the war, are instruments of the Republic's war, and have lost their way as peacekeepers. Met Letta, who shared the anti-Jedi, anti-war view. She was also disillusioned by what the war did to her. She says she attacked the Temple to make a statement. Palpatine lets her finish her speech (TV Tropes recap) and it turns opinion against the Jedi. [CONFIRMED]
- Charges dropped. The Council apologizes. Yoda offers reinstatement; Mace Windu says her ordeal counts as her Great Trial, making her a Knight. [CONFIRMED per one digest]
- Ahsoka declines and leaves the Temple at sunset; Yoda is shown looking down in shame. Anakin's last exchange: he pleads/offers the place back, she declines; one digest says he says he understands and she answers "I know". [UNSURE on exact wording; the digest's claim that Anakin offered a Padawan braid is likely wrong, Togruta Padawans in TCW do not wear braids; verify]
- Beads: the task brief asks about "beads returned." No search digest confirmed anything about beads in the finale. [UNSURE / NOT FOUND]. Ahsoka's lightsabers: a fan wiki says they stayed with the Order after she left (one says she handed them to the Council), and Anakin returned them (modified, blue per one source) during the Siege of Mandalore (TCW S7). Discrepancy on who held them. [UNSURE]

## 3. Barriss Offee
- Mirialan; Padawan of Luminara Unduli; preferred studying ancient Jedi texts in the Temple over frontline service; skilled healer, volunteered at the Temple medical clinic after Geonosis [CONFIRMED from databank/wiki digests].
- Blue lightsaber [CONFIRMED]; fought at Geonosis.
- History with Ahsoka: friends since Geonosis ("Lair of Grievous"/Second Battle of Geonosis); "Legacy of Terror" (S2E7) Luminara missing; "Brain Invaders" (S2E8): Barriss infected by Geonosian brain worm, attacked Ahsoka, they duelled; they stopped the infected ship. [CONFIRMED]
- Later (after arc): Inquisitorius in the Imperial era; wandering healer later (Ahsoka novel context) [CONFIRMED by digest].
- Personality: gentle, thoughtful, idealistic; hides resolve; disillusioned with the war.
