## 1. Fives / Tup / inhibitor chip arc (S6 E1-4)
- **"The Unknown"**
  - Battle to retake the ring station over Ringo Vinda; Admiral Trench commands the droid forces. [SEARCH]
  - Anakin, Rex and the 501st fight alongside twin Jedi Generals Tiplar and Tiplee. Tup (CT-5385) suddenly turns and shoots Tiplar at point-blank range, killing her. [SEARCH]
  - Tup is sent to Kamino for study, and his transport is attacked by Separatists. Anakin and Fives escort him, and their return trip is ambushed. [SEARCH]
  - Wookieepedia lists Tiplar as female and Tiplee as the twin; sources disagree on Tiplar's gender. [UNSURE]
- **"Conspiracy"**
  - Tup is quarantined on Kamino. Rex returns to the front, and Fives is kept on Kamino because "a virus" might have caused Tup's breakdown. [SEARCH]
  - Fives works with the medical droid AZI-3 and finds the cause: a small organic construct in Tup's brain. AZI-3 finds the chip on a deep brain scan; the Kaminoans had labelled it a growth or tumor. [SEARCH]
  - The Kaminoans (Nala Se) tried to terminate Tup and swap specimens. Fives finds the chip implanted in all clones, probably from the embryo stage. [SEARCH]
  - Removing Tup's chip kills Tup, and Fives is arrested. [SEARCH]
- **"Fugitive"**
  - Fives is held on Kamino. Shaak Ti and Nala Se decide to send the chip and the data to the Grand Republic Medical Facility on Coruscant, and Fives wants to go with them. [SEARCH]
  - Shaak Ti brings Fives to Coruscant. Nala Se drugs him during the trip and slips in an unknown injection while Shaak Ti isn't looking. [SEARCH]
  - Fives escapes custody on Coruscant. [MEM]
- **"Orders"**
  - Fives gets an audience with Palpatine and tells him about the chips. [SEARCH]
  - The meeting turns into "Fives tried to attack the Chancellor." Palpatine agrees with Nala Se that Fives has gone mad without the inhibitor chip. [SEARCH] Palpatine effectively sets Fives up by provoking him and signalling the guards. [MEM]
  - Fives flees through the Coruscant underlevels, passing the clone bar "79's" while security clones hunt him. [SEARCH] He is cornered in a warehouse and shot by Commander Fox (CC-1010) of the Coruscant Guard when he reaches for a blaster. [SEARCH]
  - He dies in Rex's arms. Paraphrase of his last message: the conspiracy runs deep, and Rex must pursue the truth about the chips and "Order 66." Exact content [UNSURE].
  - Rex records or keeps Fives' findings, and Ahsoka later uses them to remove Rex's chip in Season 7. [SEARCH] Anakin is present and troubled, but whether he fully believed Fives is [UNSURE].
- **The inhibitor chip** ("behavioral modification biochip")
  - A tiny organic/biotech implant in the brain, placed in every clone embryo as part of the Kaminoan cloning process. [SEARCH]
  - It is dormant until the Order 66 transmission activates it, overriding the clone's will. The Tup and Fives incidents are glitches or premature activations.
  - Sidious and Dooku took over the cloning project after Sifo-Dyas was killed and hid the chips from the Jedi Council. [SEARCH]
  - Exact brain region is [UNSURE]. Removal is risky: it killed Tup, but Rex later survives it, so removal is possible with correct technique.
  - Cover story: a "virus" or a "tumor" from Kamino. Nala Se and the Kaminoans lie, and the Kaminoans are working to Sidious's plan. [SEARCH]
  - Only Fives, Kix, Rex and eventually Ahsoka are on to it. [MEM]

## 2. Dooku's apprentice-hunting (Sidious's awareness)
- Dooku (Darth Tyranus) wanted to overthrow Sidious eventually. [SEARCH]
- **Ventress:** She was Dooku's assassin and acolyte. Sidious grew uneasy about her mastery of the dark side and had Dooku eliminate her (~22 BBY). She survived and returned to the Nightsisters. [SEARCH]
- **Savage Opress:** Talzin and Ventress sent Opress to Dooku as a new apprentice, secretly to kill Dooku. Dooku survived. [SEARCH]
- **Quinlan Vos (Dark Disciple novel):** The Council sends Vos and Ventress to assassinate Dooku. Vos poses as a defecting apprentice, and Dooku does tempt him. [SEARCH] Vos dies near the end of the book and Dooku survives to RotS. [MEM/UNSURE]
- Sidious's awareness: he tolerated Dooku's schemes and planned to replace him with Anakin. [SEARCH] The Dark Disciple story depends on Sidious being aware. Exact foreknowledge of each plot is [UNSURE].
