## Ch01 — The Masked Jedi
The night before the Council's dawn vote, Anakin hunts Ventress through the old munitions district to a gallery above the Dovrin Line transit platform. She's bruised, sabers stolen; she begins to say the attacker knew where to wait, then the masked figure drops in with her red sabers. Anakin glimpses Temple defensive forms. The figure throws Ventress off a ledge (fate unknown), uses nano-droids to eat a pylon; a twenty-story hab stack tilts. The figure pauses on a catwalk to watch as Anakin holds the stack while everyone escapes — no deaths. Fox logs the figure as Ventress's accomplice. In an alley Barriss unmasks, vomits, and cannot drop the sabers down an incinerator chute.

## Ch02 — Expelled
Ahsoka, in a cell at the Republic Center for Military Operations, hears her lightsabers logged as evidence in the deaths of three Coruscant Guard clones. Fox tells her about the Dovrin platform: no deaths, masked figure escaped, logged as Ventress's associate. Escorted to the Temple, she passes Barriss, whose raised hand trembles. The Council expels her so the Republic can try her; Anakin argues and loses; Plo Koon looks at the floor. Windu personally removes her Padawan beads ("someone who voted for it"). Padmé agrees to defend her. Anakin visits: Ventress is the only witness; the attacker fought with Temple training (a Soresu turn he hid from Fox); he swears to find Ventress. Alone, Ahsoka accepts the Order let her go. Fox delivers the charges: Palpatine presiding, Tarkin prosecuting, death sought.

## Ch03 — The Healer
Barriss lights an unmarked dawn candle for Jackar Bowmani each morning; she trembles, can't sleep, hides a hilt-shaped bruise on her left forearm from Luminara. Luminara tells her Tarkin seeks death — Barriss had only imagined expulsion. That night she means to destroy Ventress's sabers and mask beneath the Temple but can't crush the bled crystals; she wraps everything in a surgical drape and seals it in old crawlway C-31, three levels below the eastern foundation. Visiting Ahsoka in detention before the trial, she heals Ahsoka's wrists; Ahsoka promises never to name the friend who sent her to the warehouse, thanks her, and asks her to light candles for the three dead troopers. Barriss nearly confesses to Luminara but only says she's afraid for Ahsoka. Luminara reveals Anakin believes Ventress survived and is hunting her.

## Ch04 — The Tribunal
Padmé's POV. Ahsoka's sabers lie on the evidence table within her reach. Tarkin plays the silent footage of Letta's death; Bith forensics tech Sabo Kell matches the three troopers' wounds to sabers #417/#418; Fox testifies Ahsoka held a stolen Coruscant Guard stun baton when recaptured. Anakin testifies he didn't recognize the masked figure's style; Tarkin blames him for letting the figure escape. Padmé establishes Ahsoka was in a cell during the Dovrin attack; Tarkin replies she directed an accomplice. Day two, Ahsoka testifies, refuses to name the friend who sent her to the warehouse, glances once at the Jedi gallery, and loses her temper at Tarkin. Padmé sees the senators have decided. At recess Anakin gives Padmé the same evasive "dark, fast" answer. Closings delivered; tribunal retires; verdict expected next morning. Anakin missed closings — he messages that he has a lead on Ventress.

## Ch05 — The Chancellor's Mercy
Anakin traces the med-kit lead through Sullustan vendor Tibbo Nask to Berth Nine in the Undercroft: Ventress left Coruscant 31 hours earlier aboard the freighter Marrow Lark (Nikto captain Grell), leaving a sneering message with the Pantoran berth-master. Anakin punches a beam and races to the Senate, arriving as Ahsoka is declared guilty on four counts; Tarkin asks for death. In the Chancellor's red office Anakin begs, offers "anything"; Palpatine declines the offer, promises nothing, and asks where the Jedi were. At sentencing Palpatine pronounces death, then invokes emergency clemency powers to commute it to life imprisonment on Narkis. Ahsoka is still at "death" and breaks at "life". Anakin, overwhelmed with gratitude, lashes out at Obi-Wan in the corridor. Coda: Palpatine savors the debt, freezes gallery footage on a hooded Mirialan's guilty face, and saves the frame.

## Ch06 — Good Soldiers
Rex's POV. An hour after sentencing he receives an order to strike Ahsoka from the 501st roster. In the barracks Fives hurls his helmet into a bulkhead, Jesse is bitter, Kix recounts bacta patches; Tup presses his right temple, blames the lights for a headache, refuses a scan. Rex holds discipline but can't confirm the removal. In the rebuilt Temple hangar Anakin signs him a visitation pass — Ahsoka asked Anakin not to come again, so he won't. In her cell Ahsoka is composed for Rex's sake and gives him a last order: look after Anakin, who's not as strong as he thinks. Rex's salute stalls halfway, then completes. At dawn, at Platform Six, he watches the grey Judiciary transport leave and reckons with his own part in hunting her. Fox mentions a Force-dampening collar to be fitted in flight. Rex leaves the order unconfirmed and asks Fives to retrace that night in the lower levels.

## Ch07 — The Deep
In flight, a technician fits the Force-dampening collar under the Coruscant Guard's cold watch; Ahsoka touches the Force one last time before the blue light comes on and it goes silent. On Narkis, Commandant Ilsa Vorne — precise, never cruel, openly loyal to Tarkin, holder of the only collar control — processes her as prisoner Twenty-two fourteen (NK9-2214) and assigns cell 4-17. In the mess inmates jeer; Trandoshan Krasso, Garnac's nephew, seizes her collar over Wasskah. Tess Varro drives him off, lays out the rules, and mentions a Separatist officer held in the Well with the lights always on. That night Ahsoka dreams of the hangar, nearly claws at the collar, discovers her breathing still works without the Force, and chooses to live. Below, the Separatist prisoner looks up the shaft and salutes her; she salutes back. (End of Part One.)

## Ch08 — The Weight of Gratitude
Six weeks later on Ordaine, Anakin ignores Obi-Wan's order to wait for bombers, leaps alone onto a cliff battery, turns its gun into the rock, and takes a plasma burn from left armpit to lower ribs. Obi-Wan relays that the Council "asks" Anakin not to petition for contact with Ahsoka; Anakin coldly calls him "Master Kenobi." Rex secretly passes on Fives's lead: Rodian salvage broker Dalo Pesk (Kesh Arcade, stall 41). Anakin finds Pesk dead without a mark, stall solvent-cleaned, seven datapads and the cam core gone, hands folded — someone is cleaning up. Padmé's appeal is denied (a clemency "act of grace" can't be appealed); she warns Anakin about how much kindness he accepts from Palpatine. Palpatine hints at a postwar general amnesty and reveals Windu wrote asking him to deny Anakin's requests. At midnight a Narkis visitation permit arrives, signed by the Chancellor: "So she knows. —P." Anakin will go regardless of Ahsoka's wish.

## Ch09 — Garnac's Blood
Ahsoka's first days in the ice cut. Tess teaches the rules: bare skin never on metal, never run, exactly forty blocks, never show what you can do. Tess warns Krasso will strike in Cut Three, terrace eleven, at shift change, where a camera has been frozen two years — someone traded to get Ahsoka posted there. Krasso attacks with an ice pick; without the Force she slides under, trips him, leaves him dangling over the edge, holds a second pick to his throat — then hauls him up. Vorne gives her ten days in the Well, Krasso fourteen: "the rule is the rule." Through a vent she meets Merrick Tal (Commander, Third Raxus Picket, schoolteacher's son), who argues the Republic "needs, and someone pays." She disagrees but his words stick; she learns to meditate without the Force. On release Merrick notes the real killer knows where she is — she begins wondering who knew where she'd go.

## Ch10 — The Chancellor's Friend
A Chancellery request for "a staff matter" brings Barriss to heal aide Dace Orrin's wrist; Palpatine asks her to stay for tea. Gently, he plays a holo from a forgotten auxiliary maintenance camera at the Military Operations Center: Barriss's raised hand at the exact second Letta choked. He has sealed it, mentions later detention-level recordings he "didn't need" to watch, and shows his saved frame of her face at sentencing. He frames silence as protecting the Republic — and Ahsoka's life — and asks only "friendship": tell him what the Council fears. Telling herself the Senate should oversee the Jedi, she agrees; the warm room turns cold. She sleeps nine hours and misses the dawn candles. Luminara confides the Council's fears about Anakin; Barriss stores them. Asked if there's anything to tell, she lies easily; Luminara senses "a door that has closed." Barriss volunteers for the prison healer circuit ending at Narkis; the Chancellery approves within hours: "She will be so glad of a friendly face. —P."

## Ch11 — Visitors
Day 53 — before the 90-day rule — Anakin visits Narkis against Ahsoka's wish. Vorne supervises the two-hour visit in a Ring One room, Ahsoka bound to the table; with the collar she can't feel him in the Force. He's thinner, stiff from the Ordaine burn, praises the Chancellor's promised amnesty, rails at Windu and the Council, and unconsciously crushes a steel cup with the Force — Vorne's collar control is useless against him; Ahsoka is frightened. She refuses to name her friend, deciding the friend is in danger, not a suspect, and makes him promise not to do anything stupid. Day 92: a hauling chain snaps; Ahsoka pulls 19-year-old Rodian inmate Pell from a chute and gashes her shin. The visiting healer is Barriss, who heals her shin, shoulder and collar-nerve pain. They share the Geonosis memory; Ahsoka says she's trying not to hate the Council and warns Barriss to beware a "cleaner." Barriss leaves shaking; Ahsoka reads it as kindness. Cliffhanger: after Barriss files a note on collar nerve damage, Vorne arrives at Ahsoka's cot holding the collar control.

## Ch12 — Ringo Vinda
Rex's POV. On Ringo Vinda Station the 501st and Generals Tiplar and Tiplee take a rotating transit hub from Trench's droids; Tup keeps pressing his right temple. In the quiet after the fighting, as Tiplar kneels to check on him, Tup shoots her point-blank during the hub's sun-glare, then repeats that good soldiers follow orders. Rex steps between Tiplee's blade and Tup as Fives tackles him. On the Resolute, Kamino forbids any scans and a Judge Advocate calls it a deliberate act; within a day the troopers call Tup a traitor — Rex feels Ahsoka's verdict again, and Anakin hears the same echo. Fives insists something physical is wrong and is assigned as Tup's escort to Kamino; at the shuttle he promises he won't go quietly the way she had to. That night Rex begins an offline record and finds Kamino's no-scan order was logged before his own report. Fives's channel has gone dark.

## Ch13 — What Fives Found
Fives's POV. On Kamino, Tup dies when the "growth" is removed. Medical droid AZI-3 had secretly scanned him and found a small, clearly grown implant; Fives has himself scanned — every clone carries one from the embryo stage — and AZI-3 removes his while he's awake. Lama Su and Nala Se call it a parasite; Shaak Ti arranges for him to see the Chancellor instead. Remembering Ahsoka's trial, Fives first has AZI-3 send everything to Rex. On Coruscant Nala Se drugs him; Palpatine takes the vial, stages Fives as an attacker, and keeps the chip. Fives flees past 79's to an abandoned refinery he and Rex once explored. Rex and Anakin find him; he pleads. Fox fires; Rex knocks Fox's arm; Fives is hit in the left shoulder and falls through a grating down the drainage shaft. Fox reports him dead, body unrecoverable. In the dark below Fives clings, then loses grip and consciousness.

## Ch14 — Narrow Paths
Nineteen hours after Fives's fall, a Kaminoan report blames a rare "virus" from Ringo Vinda; numb, Anakin wants to believe it. Palpatine, a yellow bruise on one hand, is warm — says it hurts most "when the loyal turn out to be false," sees Anakin's face and apologizes, then asks whether Fives said anything. Anakin says nothing that made sense and omits Rex knocking Fox's arm (Fox omitted it too). In the rebuilt hangar Anakin lashes at Obi-Wan, who admits he doesn't believe Ahsoka is guilty, perhaps never did — enraging Anakin — then Obi-Wan's "Like you?" lands on Anakin's own silence about the Soresu turn. At 500 Republica Anakin dreams of Ahsoka under ice, her collar light dying, and tears the sheet with his mechanical hand; he tells Padmé of Palpatine's remark and says he'll seek another permit. On Serenno, Dooku watches Holonet coverage of a hearing on the collars and hires Cad Bane through a private relay.

## Ch15 — The Admiral's Visit
Opens in recollection: after Barriss's note, Vorne turned the collar down to prevent seizures — regulation, not kindness — leaving Ahsoka a faint, unusable whisper of the Force. Before Tarkin's inspection, a hot coolant line ruptures in Cut Five and an ice overhang begins to fall; Vorne releases the collar and for 4 minutes 9 seconds Ahsoka holds the ice while 23 people — prisoners, Tess, guards, a pinned Krasso — escape. She could walk out; instead she lowers the shelf. Collar back on, the whisper is thinner. Tess warns she's now "worth something different"; Krasso says his debt can't be settled. Merrick is told he'll be transferred. Privately, Tarkin tells her guilt never mattered — she was the most useful answer and is now evidence for the collar program. She sees she's a symbol, not a person. Tess's homemade scanner shows three transponderless ships waiting on the moon's far side.

## Ch16 — Bane
Cad Bane's crew cuts Narkis's power and opens Ring Four. Sergeant Holt, moving Ahsoka to a secure room, is killed by Bane on the stair. Ahsoka fights Bane without the Force; Krasso tackles Bane's Aqualish crewman Vosh to buy her a chance, but Bane's rocket boots catch her and he stuns her. Half-conscious, she sees Bane shoot Vorne, take the collar control, and destroy the office terminal keyed to it; Merrick hooded and loaded onto a separate hauler; Tess hiding among looted crates. On the Justifier, Dooku's hologram offers her a place; she refuses. Bane says he'll deliver her regardless. Tess emerges, opens Ahsoka's cuffs with her scanner coil; Ahsoka pulls the hyperdrive coupling and the ship drops out at Florrum. They flee in a pod — Bane promising to come collect — and crash-land, collar still on. Holonet bulletin: the convicted Temple bomber freed in a Separatist raid, believed to have joined Count Dooku. (End of Part Two.)

## Ch17 — Fugitive
The Council projects Ahsoka's prison number; Vorne has named Bane as the raid's leader, so Windu presumes Ahsoka is with Dooku and authorizes lethal force in the field. Anakin erupts, Force-cracking a floor glow-panel. Windu assigns Anakin and Obi-Wan with the 501st, attaching Luminara and Barriss at the Chancellor's office's suggestion; Obi-Wan says he argued against the presumption. In the hangar a composed Barriss tells Anakin how Ahsoka seemed at Narkis. Tarkin introduces Major Teva Rusk's independent Judiciary Special Recovery team, carrying a spare collar; Barriss blanches at the case, and Anakin mistakes it for shared horror. Privately Palpatine claims he tried to stop the order and asks Anakin to bring Ahsoka to him; Anakin vows not to stop to save anyone else this time. That night Rex decodes a fake Guard power-cell fault ping in their check-in cipher, signed "5554": Fives is alive.

## Ch18 — Pirates
Hondo's pirates find Ahsoka and Tess in Florrum's Red Narrows. Hondo, delighted, tallies aloud what she's worth to the Republic, Dooku and Bane (insulted the Republic hasn't posted a reward), takes Bane's coming as a personal affront, and wraps his coat around her when she shivers. Old Weequay mechanic Juko drains the collar's anti-tamper charge cell — still jolting her into a brief seizure, during which she breaks one of Tess's frostbitten fingers gripping her hand — then cuts it off. The Force floods back and she weeps; Hondo blames the fumes and keeps the collar to sell. He lends her a blue-bladed saber with a contradictory tale of winning it from a Jedi Master; through the crystal she knows he's lying and its owner was peaceful. Tess, hand in pocket, asks whether the north-tower comm dish reaches off-world. That evening Ventress walks in, hooded and sabreless, having heard there was a Jedi for sale.

## Ch19 — Old Wounds
Ventress's POV. She faces Ahsoka down in Hondo's yard, then in a storeroom describes the attacker: Jedi-trained, calm water with something dead at the bottom. Dalo Pesk had sold nano-droids to a hooded buyer with green, patterned hands and asked 2,000 credits for his stall-cam recording; Anakin's sweep forced Ventress to run, and Pesk was dead when she came back. Ahsoka visibly flinches from a thought, changes the subject, and refuses to discuss her "friend in the Temple." Pact: Ventress gets a way into the Temple (she faintly senses her sabers on Coruscant); Ahsoka gets a witness. At night Ventress watches Tess climb the north tower and an amber light start blinking. At dawn Bane attacks; Ventress fights with a rifle, crates and a geyser burst, Ahsoka with the blue saber, Hondo's roof gun cripples the Justifier. Bane, scalded, retreats — shooting Tess as he goes. Tess admits it, keeps her chit, stays with Hondo. Ahsoka sends Rex a 501st "officer cut off" code.

## Ch20 — The Dead Man
Rex's POV. The Resolute sits in its cradle at the fleet yards for 18 hours while Tarkin's office processes Rusk's collar case. Rex lies to Anakin about fetching fever shots; Anakin says the Chancellor wants Ahsoka brought to him, and Rex keeps silent about Fives and the chip. Ahsoka's Christophsis code arrives on the old 501st band: Florrum, able to fight, not alone. At 0300 Rex meets Fives in the Seventh Refinery's outflow chamber, at the bottom of the shaft he fell down — thin, feverish, his left shoulder infected. Fives explains the chips, the coming order, the Chancellor keeping his chip; Rex believes him because he won't be silent as he was at the tribunal, but won't yet involve Kix or remove his own chip. Fives will go to Ahsoka; Rex pays passage on the scrap hauler Gravel Saint from Berth Nine and replies "Hold position" via public relay. At departure Anakin says he felt Ahsoka in the Force last night, collar off. Rex realizes he's racing Anakin.

## Ch21 — Green Hands
Ahsoka and Ventress wait at "Number Nine," an abandoned mining outpost in Florrum's canyons; Ahsoka keeps veering from a thought while Ventress needles her about her "friend in the Temple." Fives arrives on one of Hondo's skiff-bikes, feverish, with the recognition line. Ahsoka eases his infected shoulder with the Force as he tells the chip story — Rex has one; the Chancellor kept Fives's — and it stuns her; Ventress mocks, then falls silent. The smell of bacta returns Barriss's shaking hands at Narkis. That night Ahsoka works the case: the comlink call that sent her to the warehouse, the cell visit before the keycard, the Soresu defense, the green patterned hands, the unasked-for fourth candle. Certain, quietly devastated, she says Barriss's name to Ventress — but her only proof is Ventress's word and a dead dealer. At "the Temple's depths" Ventress feels her sabers sharpen beneath the stone. At dawn Ahsoka feels Anakin close and turning toward her; he feels her back.

## Ch22 — The Canyon
Anakin's POV. At dawn he feels Ahsoka — and her gladness — before she shuts him out. Tarkin's beacon intercept brought the Resolute to Florrum; Rex searches Hondo's base, Barriss stays aboard, Rusk's corvette is inbound. At Number Nine Ventress fights Obi-Wan with a knife and the Force while Ahsoka draws Anakin away; he smells bacta but sees no one. In a canyon of white mineral crust he begs her to come in for the Chancellor's pardon; she answers that the Chancellor already made his choice once. She knows the culprit but won't say who, fearing what he'd do — stones lift around him as he rages. Neither saber ignites. He tells her how to evade Rusk and lets her go. To Obi-Wan he claims she lost him in the vents; the white crust on his boots says otherwise, and Obi-Wan lets the lie stand. Rusk reports a scow jumping Core-ward.

## Ch23 — The Order
Barriss's POV. Six hours out from Florrum, Palpatine reaches her in the Resolute's medbay under cover of a follow-up on Dace Orrin's wrist. If Ahsoka is cleared there will be a second trial, which his sealed footage cannot survive; he can only protect Barriss in a world with no second trial. He speaks of healers, steady hands, Anakin's rage — never "kill her." She understands, and her hands don't shake. At the Temple clinic Luminara mentions Ventress fought Obi-Wan with only a knife, recalls the old bruise, and asks directly whether Barriss has something to tell her. Barriss lies; Luminara knows, and leaves the choice to her: "I'll be in my quarters. If that changes." On the third night home Barriss cuts open crawlway C-31 and retrieves the sabers and mask. Climbing out, a short-range text arrives on her old civilian comlink's private channel: "I know. Meet me where you left me." She walks past Luminara's lit door into the rain toward the lower levels, not knowing which blade she will light.

## Ch24 — Senator Amidala's Guests
Padmé comes home to find Ventress in her reading chair, then Ahsoka, then Fives — supposedly dead. Over tea Fives explains the chips; the scale frightens her. Ahsoka names Barriss, and Padmé is horrified: Barriss sat in the gallery every day. Next day they plan around Fives's own dented helmet recorder (sealed, timestamped, tamper-evident) with Padmé as Senator-witness. Ahsoka asks her not to tell Anakin, reveals he let her go on Florrum, and chooses every word carefully when saying he "comes to see you sometimes"; Padmé agrees, hating it. At dusk the three leave; Ahsoka sends the text near the Temple. Anakin comes home, smells bacta, asks if Ahsoka has come to her; Padmé tells her first real lie to him, then a second about the "sent" signal. At second hour she slips out. Short Anakin scene: awake in the dark, he feels Ahsoka waiting far below and reaches for his boots.

## Ch25 — Where It Began
Ahsoka waits in the ruined warehouse; Fives and Padmé record from the north crane gantry, Ventress holds the south doors. Barriss enters by the west personnel door, puts on the bone-white mask, then removes it. She confesses everything: the nano-droids bought from Pesk, the hangar bombing and her reasons, choking Letta from the upper service passage, killing the three troopers with Ahsoka's sabers, the keycard and warehouse frame, and Dovrin — Anakin chose civilians over catching her. The healing visits; the fourth candle was for Jackar. "Someone" powerful holds a recording and sent her to ensure Ahsoka never testifies — she lets slip "he" but names no one. Ahsoka begs her to confess publicly; Barriss draws Ventress's red sabers instead. Mid-duel Barriss has a clean opening at Ahsoka's throat and stops. Ahsoka disarms her and refuses to kill her. The Force turns cold: Anakin's darkened presence descends the west stair.

## Ch26 — The Dark Between Stars
Anakin follows Padmé to the warehouse, finds the bone-white mask by the door, then Barriss on her knees, and understands: she was the masked figure. She admits she counted on him saving the civilians at Dovrin. He Force-chokes her, lifting her; she doesn't resist. Ahsoka steps between them — this is how it started, what Barriss did to Letta — and calls him "Skyguy"; feeling her fear of him, he lets go. (The gantry recording light goes dark during the choke — implied Padmé switched it off.) Obi-Wan and Luminara arrive; Barriss claims her bruised throat came from a fall. Fox's Coruscant Guard storm in with orders to fire; Anakin deflects a shot. Ventress seizes her sabers and Obi-Wan lets her go. Barriss confesses everything to Fox, naming no one else; Padmé hands over the sealed chip. Ahsoka gives Luminara Barriss's saber and surrenders to Anakin. Word arrives the Chancellor is grateful. Led away, Barriss asks Ahsoka to visit: "There's one more thing that's true."
