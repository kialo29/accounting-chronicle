### Ch24 — Senator Amidala's Guests (POV: Padmé)
Ahsoka, Ventress and Fives reach Coruscant; Padmé hides them, risking everything. She recognizes Fives — supposedly dead — and the scale of what's happening frightens her. Ahsoka asks her not to tell Anakin; Padmé, torn between husband and conscience, agrees and hates it. Plan: draw Barriss out and record a confession before witnesses (Fives's helmet recorder; Padmé as a Senator of standing). Padmé notices how carefully Ahsoka speaks of Anakin. That night Anakin comes home; Padmé lies to him for the first time about something that matters.

### Ch25 — Where It Began (POV: Ahsoka)
The abandoned warehouse in the lower levels. Barriss arrives masked, with Ventress's sabers, and removes the mask. The conversation: the truth, Barriss's reasons, the grief, Ahsoka's fury held in check. Barriss reveals she was sent to make sure Ahsoka never testifies. Ahsoka refuses: she won't become the thing they convicted.
