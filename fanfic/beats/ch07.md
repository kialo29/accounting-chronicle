### Ch07 — The Deep (POV: Ahsoka)
Transport to Narkis, an ice moon at the edge of Wild Space. The Force-dampening collar: the Force is gone for the first time in her life — a deafness, a grief. Processing by the Warden, Commandant Ilsa Vorne (correct, unbending, a Tarkin protégé). The prison carved into the ice: "the Deep." Inmates who hate Jedi; a Trandoshan named Krasso who knows she killed his kinsman Garnac on Wasskah. An older human smuggler, Tess Varro, gives cynical advice. First night: cold, despair, nightmares of the hangar explosion. Ends with Ahsoka choosing to survive — and noticing a Separatist prisoner held in isolation.

### Ch08 — The Weight of Gratitude (POV: Anakin)
Six weeks later. Anakin is reckless on the front lines (a brutal Outer Rim battle). Obi-Wan's concern is met with coldness. Anakin's secret investigation: the nano-droids' black-market source, a dealer in the lower levels — found dead before Anakin reaches him, scene too clean. Someone is cleaning up. Padmé's appeal is denied. Palpatine dangles hope: when the war is won, a Chancellor might grant pardons the Council would never allow. Anakin's resentment of the Council hardens.
