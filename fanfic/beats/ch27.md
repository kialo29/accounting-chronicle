### Ch27 — The Second Tribunal (POV: Padmé)
The tribunal reconvenes. Barriss's public confession, in her own words, an indictment of the war and the Jedi as much as of herself. Tarkin, cornered, pivots to charges for Ahsoka's escape and the Narkis dead. Palpatine, magnanimous, grants Ahsoka a full pardon and praises Anakin's loyalty publicly. The Senate applauds. Padmé watches Anakin glow with gratitude and Ahsoka stay perfectly still. Tarkin's quiet exchange with Palpatine afterward suggests he will be rewarded, not punished.

### Ch28 — Mirror (POV: Ahsoka)
Ahsoka visits Barriss in the same kind of cell Ahsoka once occupied. Honest, painful conversation; no forgiveness yet, but something. Barriss tells her the one thing she left out of the confession: the Chancellor knew from the beginning, and protected her to use her. Ahsoka doesn't know whether to believe it. Next morning Barriss is found dead — strangled through the Force. Records from the detention level show eleven missing minutes. Anakin was logged in the building that night (collecting Ahsoka). The Council's glances at him. Ahsoka asks him directly; he swears he didn't. Palpatine personally closes the inquiry "to protect a hero of the Republic." Ahsoka believes Anakin, and is more afraid than ever.
