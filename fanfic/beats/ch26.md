### Ch26 — The Dark Between Stars (POV: Anakin)
Anakin sensed Ahsoka on Coruscant and followed Padmé. He arrives to see Barriss on her knees and understands everything at once. Rage: he Force-chokes Barriss, lifting her — the same death Letta died. Ahsoka steps between them and reaches him: this is how it started. He lets go. The Coruscant Guard arrives under orders to fire on the fugitive; Fives must vanish before Fox sees him. Barriss stands and confesses aloud in front of Fox, Obi-Wan and Padmé, while the helmet recording runs. Ventress takes her sabers and slips away past Obi-Wan, who lets her. Ahsoka surrenders herself to Anakin.
