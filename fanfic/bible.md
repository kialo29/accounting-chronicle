# THE WRONG VERDICT — Story Bible (Book One)

## Premise
Star Wars: The Clone Wars AU, Disney canon. Everything up to the end of the Season 5 "Wrong Jedi" arc matches canon EXCEPT the moment Anakin chases the masked figure: he stops to save civilians from a collapse she triggers, and Barriss Offee escapes unidentified. With no culprit, Ahsoka Tano is expelled from the Jedi Order, convicted by a Republic military tribunal, sentenced to death, and "mercifully" commuted by Chancellor Palpatine to life imprisonment. Everything after the divergence is ours. Canon facts in notes/canon_arc.md and notes/canon_context.md are authoritative for anything before the divergence and for character backgrounds.

Book One arc: Ahsoka goes from condemned prisoner to free woman who clears her own name, refuses to return to the Order, and leaves knowing the galaxy is darker than the Jedi believe. Sequel hooks: Fives alive with knowledge of the inhibitor chips; Rex chip-free; Barriss's dying claim that the Chancellor knew; Anakin indebted to Palpatine and quietly suspected by the Council; Dooku's ambitions; Padmé's secret.

## Themes
- What a verdict does to a person, and to the people who stood by. Guilt vs. blame.
- Institutions that need someone to be guilty more than they need the truth (Senate, Order, army).
- Loyalty vs. obedience (Rex, Fives, Anakin, Barriss).
- Mirrors: Barriss visits Ahsoka in a cell → Ahsoka visits Barriss in a cell. Letta strangled in custody → Barriss strangled in custody. Anakin chose to save lives instead of catching Barriss → that choice cost Ahsoka everything, and he must live with it.
- Anakin's fall, accelerated but not completed: gratitude to Palpatine is the chain. Ahsoka pulls him back once.

## Voice & Style Rules (follow exactly)
- Third person limited, past tense. ONE POV character per scene; switch POV only at a scene break. The chapter's listed POV is dominant.
- Scene breaks: a line containing only `* * *`.
- Chapter heading format: `# Chapter N: Title` then a blank line. No author's notes inside chapter files.
- Tone: grounded, cinematic, emotionally serious, with the dry humor Star Wars characters actually use (Anakin's sarcasm, Obi-Wan's understatement, Hondo's theatrics, Ventress's venom, Rex's deadpan). Not purple. Clear, vivid, concrete sensory detail. Short paragraphs in action; longer interiority in quiet scenes.
- Rating T/M blend: violence is real and has weight, deaths matter, dark themes (despair, imprisonment, betrayal, choking, torture-adjacent cruelty) allowed. NO lingering gore, no graphic mutilation, no sexual content, mild in-universe profanity only ("kriff", "frag", "blast", "stars", "hells").
- Use in-universe vocabulary: standard time units ("hours", "days" are fine), credits, comlink, holo, Holonet, datapad, blaster, durasteel, hyperspace, "the Force", "Master", "Padawan", "Commander", "General". No Earth references (no God, no Jesus, no "OK Google", no Earth animals beyond those that exist in-universe; avoid "minutes"-era clocks is NOT necessary — minutes/hours are fine).
- Nicknames are canon-true: Anakin calls Ahsoka "Snips"; she calls him "Skyguy" (rarely now, and it hurts when she does); Rex calls her "Commander" even after she has no rank; clones call Anakin "General" or "sir"; Obi-Wan calls Anakin "Anakin"; Padmé calls Anakin "Ani" only in private.
- NEVER quote or closely paraphrase dialogue from the films, TV episodes, or novels. All dialogue is original. Canon events can be referenced in narration or memory, in our own words.
- Show emotion through action, body (Ahsoka's montrals/lekku, Anakin's mechanical hand, Rex's helmet), and subtext. Avoid characters explaining their feelings in speeches; avoid therapy-speak and modern slang ("process", "trauma", "valid", "okay?" as a tic).
- Avoid fanfic clichés: no "orbs", no "the Togruta", "the older man", "the blonde" as name substitutes — use names/pronouns. No eye-color epithets. Minimal adverbs on dialogue tags; prefer "said".
- Each chapter: open with a concrete hook in the first paragraph; end on a turn, reveal, or cliffhanger that makes the next chapter irresistible. 2–4 scenes per chapter.
- Length: ~3,800 words per chapter (minimum 3,500).

## Characters (as of the divergence, ~19 BBY)
**Ahsoka Tano** — Togruta, ~17. Former Padawan of Anakin Skywalker. Twin lightsabers, reverse grip (green main blade, yellow-green shoto) — confiscated as evidence. Brave, quick, proud, compassionate, stubborn, sarcastic under pressure. Her deepest wound: the Order she loved let her go. Arc: despair → endurance (finding stillness without the Force in prison) → agency (proving her innocence) → choice (refusing to return). She never becomes cruel; anger is real but she chooses mercy. In prison she learns how the galaxy treats people without power.

**Anakin Skywalker** — Jedi Knight, General of the 501st, secretly married to Padmé. Loves Ahsoka fiercely as a little sister/student. Ch1 choice haunts him: he could have caught the bomber. Arc: guilt → rage at the Council → deepening gratitude to Palpatine → hunting Ahsoka to protect her → nearly kills Barriss (Force choke) → Ahsoka stops him → grateful again to Palpatine for her pardon; quietly suspected by the Council after Barriss's death. He did NOT kill Barriss. Mechanical right hand; dark gloves. Humor turns brittle as the book goes on.

**Barriss Offee** — Mirialan Jedi Padawan of Luminara Unduli, healer and gifted duelist, diamond-pattern facial markings, green skin, dark hood. Believes the Jedi have become soldiers of a war machine; bombed the Temple hangar to make them see it, framed Ahsoka, killed Letta Turmond with the Force in custody. She is a tragic figure, not a cackling villain: genuinely ideological, genuinely guilty, sleepless. Hides Ventress's sabers instead of destroying them (her unconscious door back). Becomes Palpatine's informant through blackmail-by-kindness. Refuses at the last to kill Ahsoka. Confesses publicly. Tells Ahsoka privately that the Chancellor knew. Dies in custody, Force-strangled (by Sidious; never shown on-page until the epilogue hints).

**Captain Rex** — Clone Captain, 501st. Steady, dry, loyal to his people beyond his orders. Arc: obedience → doubt → active, secret defiance; removes his chip at the end (Kix performs it). Still calls her "Commander".

**Fives (CT-5555)** — ARC trooper, 501st. Passionate, stubborn, brave. Discovers the inhibitor chips after Tup. Survives Fox's shot in our AU (shoulder wound, fall into the lower levels), officially dead. Becomes Ahsoka's companion; leaves with her at the end.

**Padmé Amidala** — Senator of Naboo, Ahsoka's defense counsel at the tribunal. Principled, politically sharp, brave. Secretly married to Anakin. Shelters fugitives in Part Three; lies to Anakin once and hates it. (Do NOT reveal pregnancy on-page; epilogue hint only.)

**Obi-Wan Kenobi** — Jedi Master, Council member. Dry, careful, loving toward Anakin, increasingly doubtful of the verdict; lets Ventress go at the end; knows Anakin lied in the canyon.

**Chancellor Palpatine** — presides over the tribunal; warm, grandfatherly, regretful on the surface. Every act is manipulation toward Anakin. Never revealed as Sidious to any character. Only the brief coda in Ch05 and the epilogue are from his POV, and even there he is called "the Chancellor"/"Palpatine"/"Sidious" sparingly and chillingly.

**Admiral Wilhuff Tarkin** — prosecutor; cold, precise, contemptuous of the Jedi; believes in order over truth. Not punished.

**Asajj Ventress** — ex-Sith assassin, now bounty hunter. Pale, bald, Dathomirian, curved twin red lightsabers (stolen by Barriss in the warehouse). Venomous, funny, wounded, self-interested — and secretly respects Ahsoka. Wants her sabers back and to repay the humiliation.

**Luminara Unduli** — Barriss's master, Mirialan, serene, Council-loyal. Suspects too late.

**Plo Koon** — Kel Dor Master who found Ahsoka as a child. Voted to expel; regrets it. Calls her "Little 'Soka".

**Mace Windu / Yoda** — Council leadership. Windu hard-line; Yoda troubled and sorrowful.

**Commander Fox** — Coruscant Guard, by-the-book, follows orders.

**Count Dooku** — seeks a secret apprentice to overthrow his Master; hires Cad Bane behind Sidious's back.

**Cad Bane** — Duros bounty hunter, wide-brimmed hat, breathing tubes, twin pistols, rocket boots, drawl. Ship: per notes.

**Hondo Ohnaka** — Weequay pirate of Florrum, theatrical, mercenary, oddly fond of Ahsoka (Younglings arc history). Collects lightsabers. Lends Ahsoka a blue-bladed saber.

### Original characters
**Commandant Ilsa Vorne** — Warden of Narkis Penitentiary ("the Deep"). Fifties, human, iron-grey hair, impeccable uniform, Tarkin protégé. Not sadistic — rule-bound, cold, fair by her own lights. Releases Ahsoka's collar briefly to save lives in Ch15.
**Tess Varro** — human smuggler, sixties, life sentence (killed a Hutt enforcer). Cynical, funny, maternal in a rough way. Escapes with Ahsoka; later sells her location to Bane for freedom, admits it, is left behind. Not evil; just survivor-shaped.
**Krasso** — Trandoshan inmate, kinsman of Garnac (whom Ahsoka killed on Wasskah). Wants revenge; after she spares him and saves his life, grudging debt.
**Merrick Tal** — CIS officer from Raxus, captured, idealist, articulate; voices the Separatist critique. Taken by Bane's crew in the raid toward Dooku (fate unresolved — sequel thread).

## Settings
- **Coruscant**: Jedi Temple (memorial candles, the rebuilt hangar, Council chamber, lower maintenance crawlways beneath the Temple), Senate tribunal chamber, Chancellor's office (red, warm, too warm), 500 Republica (Padmé's apartment), lower levels / industrial districts (rain, neon, steam, the abandoned warehouse where it began), Republic detention center (see notes for canon name).
- **Narkis**: ice moon at the edge of Wild Space. Republic Penal Facility Narkis-Nine, called "the Deep" — cell blocks cut into glacier ice, a tibanna-refinery and ice-mining labor yard, perpetual blue twilight, cold always. Guards are Republic Judiciary personnel plus a small clone detachment. Prisoners wear grey thermals. Force-users wear a Force-dampening collar (grey metal, a blue status light; when active the Force is silent — like going deaf).
- **Florrum**: Hondo's pirate base, sulfur-yellow sky, geysers, canyons.
- **Serenno**: Dooku's palace (brief).

## Canon anchors (pre-divergence facts — use these; do not contradict)
- Temple hangar bombing: six Jedi killed (incl. Jedi Knight Tutso Mara) plus clones and workers. Bomber Jackar Bowmani, a hangar worker, unknowingly fed explosive nano-droids by his wife Letta Turmond, an anti-war activist. Barriss supplied/guided Letta.
- Letta was held by Tarkin at the Republic military base (Republic Center for Military Operations, Coruscant). She asked to speak only to Ahsoka; Fox took Ahsoka's comlink and lightsabers at the door. Letta said a Jedi was behind it and was Force-choked from a distance (by Barriss) before naming them. Silent security footage made it look as if Ahsoka did it. Fox arrested Ahsoka.
- In her cell, Barriss visited her once. Later a keycard was left; Ahsoka thought Anakin left it. Barriss had used Ahsoka's confiscated lightsabers to kill three Coruscant Guard clones outside the cells. Ahsoka escaped, was found among the dead, fled into the lower levels, pursued by Fox, Anakin, Rex.
- In the underworld Ventress found her; they allied. Ahsoka contacted Barriss (her "friend in the Temple"), who told her where the nano-droids came from: an old munitions warehouse in the lower levels. There a masked figure in a Ventress-like mask knocked Ventress out, stole Ventress's two curved-hilt RED lightsabers, fought Ahsoka; Ahsoka was found amid nano-droid crates and recaptured. Ventress escaped.
- Tarkin demanded the Council expel Ahsoka for a Republic military tribunal (Palpatine presiding, Tarkin prosecuting seeking death, Padmé defending; senator-jurors incl. Riyo Chuchi, Mon Mothma, Bail Organa). The Council (Yoda, Windu, Ki-Adi-Mundi, Plo Koon, Saesee Tiin, Obi-Wan, Shaak Ti etc.) expelled her; Obi-Wan argued against.
- DIVERGENCE: in canon Anakin found Ventress, learned of the stolen sabers and the call to Barriss, and caught Barriss. In our story, Barriss (masked) attacks that meeting, Ventress is driven off before saying anything useful, and Anakin chooses to save civilians from a collapse instead of catching the bomber (Ch01). Nobody identifies Barriss.
- Barriss: Mirialan, green skin, diamond facial tattoos, blue lightsaber, healer at the Temple medical clinic, Padawan of Luminara. Friends with Ahsoka since Geonosis (the brain-worm incident: infected Barriss attacked Ahsoka; Ahsoka saved her).
- Ahsoka: ~17, Togruta, found by Plo Koon at age three. Green lightsaber + yellow-green shoto, reverse grip. Recent history: Onderon (Lux, Steela, Saw), guarding younglings on Ilum and fighting Hondo/Grievous on Florrum (Hondo likes her).

Kamino: medical droid AZI-3, Nala Se, Lama Su, Shaak Ti; the inhibitor chip — a small organic implant in every clone's brain from embryo stage, cover story "virus"/"tumor"; removing Tup's killed him; Fives removes his own. Fives taken to Coruscant, drugged by Nala Se, meets Palpatine, is framed as attacking him, flees through the underlevels past the clone bar 79's to a warehouse; Fox shoots him. (AU: he survives.)
