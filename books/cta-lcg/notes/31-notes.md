## Fact-check flags

1. **Bible flag 53 (RH passive; RC's first-year QIP position): RESOLVED.** s 18F conditions seen in a legislation.gov.uk extract (S1). RC's QIP status follows R1 (AP1 divisor 1: not large) and, for AP2, the reg 3(5) grace (CTM92530). The chapter adds (new) that the grace applies in **AP2** and QIPs start in **AP3**, relying on HMRC's extract wording and a practitioner example that the grace year counts as a year of being large. **Reviewer: confirm SI 1998/3175 reg 3(5) text** (open item 1 in the rulings file is the same regulation).
2. **Bible flag 54 (opening negative amount): RESOLVED (V-HMRC).** INTM284020 "not matched by subsequent profits"; on the TKS figures the losses are matched by Year 3 (+£10,000): nil ONA whatever the window.
3. **s 18F (passive) conditions on cash:** the text read literally excludes any asset other than subsidiary shares; commentators call this hard to apply where a holding company holds cash (S1, secondary forum opinion). The chapter tells RH to hold no bank balance, which is the safe reading.
4. **CFC excluded territories and Ireland: UNVERIFIED** (search returned nothing conclusive on the current SI 2012/3024 schedule; old-regime removal in 2002 is secondary). Not relied on; labelled in both editions. Bible flag 39 (excluded territories regulations not opened) **remains open**.

The £5,000 top-up is a labelled simplification (no SBIE; Irish ETR assumed 12.5% for the jurisdiction, blending with TIL). Who collects Irish top-up is left to Irish law (consistent with chapter 30; bible flag 38 still open).
