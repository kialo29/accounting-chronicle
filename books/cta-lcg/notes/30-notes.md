## Fact-check flags

1. **Bible flag 49 (UTPP ETMO computation): RESOLVED (V-HMRC).** INTM489135 confirms the corresponding amount is the other party's tax on the corresponding profits and that **exactly 80% is not an ETMO**; so Vallaria (20% v 25%) never meets gate 1 on the story's simplified figures. Statutory text of ss 217C–217D seen only in the draft (April 2025) and HMRC manual extracts; the enacted section numbering (s 217C conditions; s 217D ETMO) follows HMRC's citations. Law sheet 3 says "ss 217C–217E": the reading edition cites "ss 217C–217D" for the conditions. Reviewer: confirm enacted numbering.
2. **Bible flag 37 (threshold wording): carried, sharpened.** Statute s 129 "exceeds" (LS3 V); **HMRC's manual MTT01100 also says "exceeds"**; only the GOV.UK "Check if you need to report" guidance says "or more". Taught as such.
3. **Bible flag 48 (side-by-side): RESOLVED as "proposed, not law".** In the draft FB 2026-27 (13 July 2026; GOV.UK policy paper); OECD package January 2026 (secondary: 5 January). The "US-parented groups" safe harbour is from KPMG (secondary) and labelled "according to professional commentary". The script's description ("designed, as its name suggests, to let some countries' own minimum tax systems operate alongside the GloBE rules") is the book's characterisation.
4. **Bible flag 38 (QDMTT and CFC; Irish QDMTT): not resolved; avoided.** The chapter says who collects any Irish top-up "depends on Irish law, which this paper does not examine"; no Irish law stated. Whether a foreign QDMTT is local or creditable tax for the CFC rules is not discussed.
5. ***Glencore* Court of Appeal outcome: UNVERIFIED (order not seen).** Both editions say only that Glencore took the matter to the Court of Appeal and that later commentary cites the decision for the alternative-remedy principle. Script says the case is "decided by the High Court in twenty seventeen". No judicial words quoted.
6. **DPT announcement (3 December 2014, Osborne, "Google tax"):** secondary sources only (consistent across four). The DPT rate "set above corporation tax on purpose" is the book's characterisation (the 2015 CT main rate was not re-verified and is not stated).
7. **SBIE:** taught in principle only (HMRC MTT32000 overview); percentages omitted. The Tarnmoor figures ignore it (labelled), so the £102,000 / £66,000 top-ups are upper-bound simplifications. TCM has an office and staff in Marrovia, so a real SBIE would reduce them.
8. **GloBE deferred tax and full expensing:** "the GloBE rules count deferred tax as well" is consistent with chapter 6 ("their own deferred tax") and the covered-taxes concept; specific GloBE deferred tax mechanics (recapture, rate cap) not verified and not taught.
9. **Transitional CbCR safe harbour applied to Tarnmoor:** the harbour's real window closes with APs commencing on or before 31 December 2026. The story periods have no calendar date; the chapter says "we run the numbers on this year's rules" and also teaches the window with a labelled real-calendar example. A reviewer may prefer saying the harbour would not be available in the story's (later) years; Vallaria's ETR of 20% gives no top-up either way, so no number depends on it.
