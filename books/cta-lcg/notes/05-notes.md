- *HMRC v NCL Investments Ltd* [2022] UKSC 9: judgment by Lord Hamblen and Lady Rose (Lord Reed, Lord Briggs, Lord Sales agreeing); IFRS 2 debits deductible; HMRC lost on four grounds at every level (FTT, UT, CA, SC). Status V (press summary).
