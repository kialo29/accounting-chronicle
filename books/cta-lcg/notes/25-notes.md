## Fact-check flags

## Contradictions

- **Ledger §7 GY6 / plan ch 25 brief: "a Marrovian customer of TEL pays in a currency that cannot be transferred during GY6: TEL claims within 2 years; taxed when remittable".** The 2-year claim is Part 18 (s 1275), which applies to income arising outside the UK; a receipt of TEL's UK trade is relieved under **CTA 2009 s 173** (deduction not creating a loss; withdrawal under s 175 when remittable), per BIM42750. The chapter teaches the trading relief for TEL and uses Part 18 for a labelled contrast (foreign interest). Amount fixed at **£400,000** (labelled example). Suggest the ledger wording be changed to "TEL's s 173 deduction; withdrawn in GY7 (s 175)".
- **Plan §3.16 / bible "reform of the foreign PE exemption" (draft Finance Bill 2026-27)**: now identified as a **compulsory** exemption for APs beginning on or after 1 January 2027 (proposed). Not a contradiction, an update.
- **Plan glossary list "small companies limited to full treaty territories"** consistent. **Plan "from the AP after the election"**: refined to "from the relevant day, the start of the next AP as expected when the election is made" (HMRC).
- No conflict with chapters 1–14 found (chapters 8 and 11 describe Calder's GY3 sale to TVS; chapter 11 mentions the s 18A intangibles exclusion, consistent).
