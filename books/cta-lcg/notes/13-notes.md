## Fact-check flags

1. **CONTRADICTION WITH LEDGER (top flag): TPLC's surrenderable management expenses.** CTA 2010 s 105(3A) (verified via search extracts of legislation.gov.uk and CTM80142) adds CFC chargeable profits apportioned to the surrendering company to the profit-related threshold. TPLC has TCM's £825,000 apportioned each year GY1–GY4 (ledger). So the maximum surrender is **£5,075,000 (GY1)** and **£5,475,000 (GY2)**, not £5.9m and £6.3m; **£825,000 a year is carried forward** (s 371UD relief against the CFC charge was repealed). The chapter teaches this and labels the ledger's figure as "excess over gross profits". Knock-on (computed in Python, not written into my chapter): TEL GY1 group relief from TPLC £11.665m (5.075 + 6.59), TEL TTP **£10,535,000**, CT **£2,633,750** (ledger £9.71m / £2,427,500); TEL GY2 group relief £17.095m, TTP **£8,905,000**, CT **£2,226,250** (ledger £8.08m / £2,020,000), before any Part 5A claim of TPLC's carried-forward £825,000. GY5 (TCM apportionment £1,275,000) would cap TPLC's surrender similarly. **Ruling needed.** Options: (a) adopt the corrected figures in chapters 7, 15, 28 (tax-EBITDA is unaffected); (b) keep TEL's figures by raising TPLC's ME to £8.825m (GY1) and £9.325m (GY2) — but then the *Centrica* allocation and ME totals change; (c) keep everything and treat the cap as a labelled hypothetical (not recommended: the CFC charge is a story fact, so the story would be wrong in law). I recommend (a). **Resolved by continuity ruling R3: option (a) adopted; ledger amended.**
2. **Recharge characterisation (bible flag 55): resolved for the numbers.** The book treats TPLC's recharge as non-trading income (s 979, income not otherwise charged; label: the book's assumption). If it were trading income, trading profit £100,000, ME £6.0m, threshold £925,000, surrender still **£5,075,000** and c/f £825,000: no numerical effect. The s 979 characterisation itself is not verified against HMRC guidance (search found CTM08060 categories only).
