## Fact-check flags

1. **CONTRADICTION (stamp duty on the TAL sale).** Ledger §7 GY6 and plan ch 20/21 give Brennock's stamp duty as **£240,000** (0.5% × £48.0m). Under the contingency principle (STSM021120, verified extract) the earn-out's stated cap of £6.0m is also chargeable: **£270,000** in total (£240,000 + £30,000). Both editions teach £270,000 and show the £240,000 component. **Fix needed:** ledger GY6 line and chapter 21 (and ch 31 if it lists stamp duty) to £270,000, or recast the earn-out as uncapped (formula only, no stated maximum), in which case £240,000 stands and this chapter's figure must change. Recommend £270,000 (it teaches a real trap).
2. **New stamp duty on BSL's convertible notes: £9,000** (0.5% × £1.8m; FA 1986 s 79(5), verified). Chapter 21 should list it alongside £111,000.
3. **Earn-out receipts after an SSE-exempt sale:** UNVERIFIED (search returned nothing conclusive). Both editions say only that later receipts are disposals of the right, not of shares, and leave the taxability to chapter 18. Bible flag 28 remains open.
4. ***Marren v Ingles* court:** House of Lords per secondary sources (search 2); citation [1980] 1 WLR 983, 54 TC 76 from LS2 (S/V). Bible flag 28 (court) partly resolved (secondary).
5. ***M Group* reported gain "about £53m"**: from LS2 (R, TKS-verified £53.2m), not re-seen in this session's extracts. Labelled "reported".
6. **Para 15A and the pre-incorporation part of the investee trading test (para 19):** the chapter states only that the investee must trade immediately after the sale when para 15A is relied on (LS2, V). How para 19's "qualifying period" treats the months before TAL existed was not verified; not taught.
7. **ESC D33** status: still published per CG14825 extracts; a 2016 consultation on legislating it has not (per commentary) produced change. Taught as "HMRC's concession; in practice".
8. **SP 3/93 arrangements timing** for group relief (as opposed to consortia): the agreement-in-principle dating is from CTM80625 (consortium chapter); taught with that label. Chapter 15 owns the point.
9. **CTA 2010 ss 139–142** cited for overlapping periods (section numbers from memory of Part 5 Ch 4; not opened). Chapter 15 should confirm.
10. **TAL QIP thresholds** (£125,000 / £1,666,667 / £833,333 for an 11-month AP with divisor 11) assume the £1.5m, £20m and £10m figures are all divided and time-apportioned, as chapter 3 and R1 did for Calder's 9-month AP (HMRC's view, CTM92530). Chapter 21 should not state that the hive-down was made with a sale already arranged.
