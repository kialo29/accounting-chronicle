## Bible update

**Cast (real cases):**
- *Barclays Mercantile Business Finance Ltd v Mawson* [2004] UKHL 51: ~£91m purchase of part of an Irish Sea gas pipeline leased back to Bord Gáis Éireann; HL allowed the lessor's allowances; used as background to the FA 2006 LFL regime (ch 9). Status S.
- *Kenmir Ltd v Frizzell* [1968] 1 All ER 414 at 418, Widgery J (going concern test; employment case) (ch 9). V via *Haymarket*.
- *Haymarket Media Group Ltd v HMRC* [2022] UKFTT 168 (TC), TC08495, 18 May 2022; Judge Heidi Poon and Member Julian Sims; VAT TOGC on the sale of Teddington Studios; appeal dismissed (ch 9). V (citation) / S (facts).

**Glossary terms explained (ch 9):** structures and buildings allowance (AT depth); allowance statement; fixture; pooling requirement; fixed value requirement; disposal value statement requirement; s 198 election (and s 199); past-owner cap (s 185); long funding lease; funding lease (finance lease, lease payments 80%, useful economic life 65% tests); short lease (CA: 7 years or less); succession (s 265); connected-party succession election (ss 266–267); connected plant sale (ss 217–218); alternative amount election (s 569); contribution allowance; special tax site; allowance buying (relevant excess of allowances; qualifying change).

**Established facts fixed (with source):**
- SBA purchases: s 270BD (developer, unused: price paid); s 270BC (non-developer, unused: lower of price and construction cost); s 270BE (developer, used) — legislation.gov.uk / CA93650 / CA93500 extracts. V.
- SBA rate 2% from 29 October 2018; 3% from 1 April 2020 (CT), 6 April 2020 (IT) — GOV.UK. V.
- Allowance statement contents (s 270IA), FA 2022 addition, s 270IA(5) special tax site amount. V.
- SBA land and land alteration exclusion; site preparation includes demolition; whole demolition ends SBA, claimed SBA added to gains consideration — CA94010 / CA93150. V (HMRC guidance).
- Contributions: s 532 general; s 536; s 537 (unconnected); s 538A (SBA). V.
- Allowance buying: CAA Part 2 Ch 16A (ss 212A–212S), FA 2010 s 26 and Sch 4, from 21 July 2009; s 212LA thresholds (FA 2013). V (explanatory notes / statute extracts).
- Freeport SBA sunset: 30 Sept 2031 (England), 30 Sept 2034 (Scottish Green Freeports, Welsh Freeports, Investment Zones). V (GOV.UK).
- s 70DA: no AIA/FYA for transfer and long funding leaseback. V.
