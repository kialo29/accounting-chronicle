# Pronunciation guide

*As built, 9 October 2026. Merged from every chapter's notes (prologue, chapters 1–32, framing pieces), the TKS names carried in the starting bible, and the bible's own list; deduplicated and alphabetical. For the text-to-speech voice that reads the audio scripts (`chapters/*.txt`). Capitals mark the stressed syllable. Where two notes files disagreed, the more common form is given (Brackenwell, Frizzell, Widgery, Hamblen, Ingles); uncertain pronunciations are marked.*

**Conventions the scripts already follow** (writer brief §7; plan §11): acronyms that practitioners say as letters are written spaced in the scripts ("H M R C", "C F C", "S D L T", "E B I T D A") and declared at first use; only **GAAR**, **DOTAS** and **GloBE** are spoken as words; sections are read normally, four-digit sections in pairs ("section eleven forty one"), and the practitioner names "one seven one", "one seven nine", "four four one", "one six four A", "eighteen A". Numbers and years are written out in words. The TKS list (Vallaria, Marrovia, Ridgeway, De Beers, Loreburn, Calder) is included below.

**If a voice still stumbles:** most TTS engines accept a substitution lexicon; the "Say it" column can be pasted into one. Test the invented names (Tarnmoor, Brackenwell, Vallaria, Marrovia) and the judges' names (Nugee, Falk, Lewison) first: they recur most.
