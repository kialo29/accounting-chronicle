# Author notes: fact-check flags (as built, 9 October 2026)

*Every point in *The Living Law: Groups and Borders* that is still open, or rests on a single, secondary or unverified source, gathered from all notes files (`notes/00-notes.md` to `notes/32-notes.md`, `notes/framing-notes.md`), the continuity rulings' open items (`continuity-rulings.md`) and the seven technical reviews (`review/review-A.md` to `review/review-G.md`). Deduplicated: a point used in several chapters appears once, under the chapter that owns it, with the others listed. Points that rulings or reviews resolved are not repeated here (see `book-bible.md` §5.2 for the resolution of every planning flag).*

**How verification was done.** WebFetch was unavailable throughout the build, so primary sources were read through **WebSearch extracts** (legislation.gov.uk, GOV.UK manuals, Find Case Law, CIOT pages), often truncated, plus the research files' verified items. The OECD's website could not be opened at all.

**Labels used in the text** (and below): "the law says" (statute seen); "HMRC's view / HMRC's guidance says" (manual seen); "as reported / commentators say" (secondary); "this book's reading" (reasoned, not confirmed); "in outline" (stated at a high level from standard knowledge); "proposed, not law"; "not settled"; "check before you rely on it".

**Priority.** **High** = could cost exam marks (or the sitting) if wrong. **Medium** = a taught rule or detail that could mislead in practice or in a narrower question. **Low** = background, history, case colour, story detail or source tidiness.
